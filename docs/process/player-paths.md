# Player-path scenarios

Each production chain of the game has one scenario that plays it from player commands alone ([testing-strategy.md](testing-strategy.md) § Player-path scenarios). This list says which chains are covered.

| Chain | From the player's commands to | Scenario |
|---|---|---|
| Food | a farm plot planted, harvested, cooked and the meal eaten | [`pp_food`](../../test/scenarios/pp_food.scenario.ts): a cave plot of cavewheat, a kitchen cooking it; a dwarf eats a cavewheat meal on day 22 |
| Drink | plants brewed in a still into barrels and drunk | [`pp_drink`](../../test/scenarios/pp_drink.scenario.ts): the wagon's drink forbidden, a stockpile that wants two barrels, a carpenter making barrels, a still brewing the wagon's dimcaps; a dwarf drinks the brew from the stockpile (day 16), though the brook's water is near |
| Crafts and forge | ore mined, smelted, forged, and the goods stored | fails: [T-337](../archive/tasks/T-337-builder-shut-inside-a-finished-workshop.md). [`pp_forge`](../../test/scenarios/pp_forge.scenario.ts): the Miner's Guild digs to galena, smelts lead and forges lead crafts into the stockpile by day 5, but the forge's builder is left on one of its impassable corners and the invariant check fails |
| Hospital | a wounded dwarf diagnosed and treated with supplies hauled into the hospital chest | fails: [T-336](../archive/tasks/T-336-hospital-chest-never-stocked-from-the-wagon.md). [`pp_hospital`](../../test/scenarios/pp_hospital.scenario.ts): a hospital zone with a bed and a carpenter-made chest; the wagon's cloth and thread never reach the chest in 28 days |
| Trade | goods hauled to the depot, a broker trading with a caravan | [`pp_trade`](../../test/scenarios/pp_trade.scenario.ts): a depot, the mason as broker, the wagon's bars, mechanism and leather sold to the Hillfolk (their arrival brought forward to day 6) for a cloak that is hauled to the stockpile on day 6 |
| Defence | a squad equipped from the stockpile, answering an alert and killing a hostile animal | [`pp_defence`](../../test/scenarios/pp_defence.scenario.ts): the Warband on a forest with no trait, woodcutting off, two axe dwarves take the wagon's axes from the stockpile; a hearth alert and a kill order for each dangerous animal that can walk to the wagon, and a soldier kills a wolf on day 29 (the scenario waits two seasons: arrivals move with the job timeline) |
| Pasture | tame grazers fed on a pasture zone through a season | fails: [T-335](../archive/tasks/T-335-grazers-starve-beside-unreachable-pasture-grass.md). [`pp_pasture`](../../test/scenarios/pp_pasture.scenario.ts): the Farmstead's six grazers on a 320-tile meadow pasture; all starve by day 12 beside a grass tile they cannot reach |

A chain is covered when its scenario exists, passes, and stages nothing that the chain produces or delivers. A chain that cannot be played from commands yet is marked "fails" with the task that records the fault; its scenario carries that task id as its `todo` and is reported as todo until the task closes.

The scenarios share `test/scenarios/player-path.ts`. Each plays a compact 48 × 48 region generated from a region spec, as the three-year fortress does: a temperate forest Serene Vale at peril 0 (the defence path drops the trait), with a brook, wealth 2 and mixed geology. A Serene Vale has the biome's wild animals but no wolves, bears or snow leopards; wolves kill dwarves of a seven-dwarf start in its second week, which is the defence chain's question and not the others'. Every scenario runs in `npm test` (the normal tier) and ends with the invariant checker.
