# 10 Dwarves

**Owner:** `systems/dwarves` · **Depends on:** [`../01-architecture/data-model.md`](../01-architecture/data-model.md), [11-jobs-and-labor.md](11-jobs-and-labor.md), [14-buildings-and-rooms.md](14-buildings-and-rooms.md), [15-happiness-and-morale.md](15-happiness-and-morale.md), [16-combat.md](16-combat.md), [17-health-and-medicine.md](17-health-and-medicine.md), [22-pathfinding-and-spatial.md](22-pathfinding-and-spatial.md)

## Purpose

Make dwarves people. A dwarf has a body with attributes, a mind with skills, needs and a personality, relationships, a daily rhythm, and a title if the player gives one. This system owns the top-level decision of what a dwarf does next and hands the details to jobs (11), the military (16) and medicine (17).

## Concepts

- **Attribute**: Strength, Agility, Toughness, Endurance, Willpower, Wits, Charm; 0–100.
- **Skill**: 0–`SKILL_MAX_LEVEL` with experience. The skill list is content; the design fixes the list in `03-content/skills-and-professions.md` with about forty skills across mining, wood, stone, crafts, farming, animals, food, metal, textiles, medicine, mechanics, trade, and combat.
- **Need**: hunger, thirst, rest, social, comfort, purpose, joy; 0 (satisfied) to `NEED_MAX`.
- **Personality trait**: three per dwarf from the trait table; each trait scales certain thoughts and biases behavior.
- **Behavior**: the state the dwarf's brain is in: `flee`, `retreat`, `need`, `duty`, `patient`, `work`, `social`, `idle`.
- **Title**: Thane, Warden, Broker, Chief Medic.
- **Child**: a dwarf under `ADULT_AGE_YEARS`.

## Rules

### Attributes

- Rolled at creation (02 for the party; 21 for immigrants) around `ATTRIBUTE_BASELINE`.
- Drift: every `SkillLevelUp` adds `ATTRIBUTE_DRIFT` to the attribute linked to that skill in the skill table (mining → Strength and Endurance; crafts → Agility and Wits; combat → per weapon; social → Charm), capped at `ATTRIBUTE_MAX`. Nothing lowers attributes.
- Uses: Strength scales melee damage and carry speed; Agility scales hit and dodge and movement; Toughness scales max blood and wound resistance; Endurance slows rest and hunger growth; Willpower resists pain and grief; Wits scales skill experience; Charm scales social relief and trade.

### Skills and experience

- Each completed job grants `xp = jobDef.xp × (100 + Wits) / 150` to the job's skill; sparring and training grant per session (16); medicine per treatment (17).
- Level thresholds: `xpToNext(level) = 100 + level × 60` (level 0→1 needs 100, 19→20 needs 1240; the full ladder is 1,900 + 11,400 = about 13,000 xp).
- Tier names by level (content): Dabbling 1, Novice 2–3, Adequate 4–5, Competent 6–7, Skilled 8–9, Proficient 10–11, Talented 12–13, Adept 14–15, Expert 16–17, Master 18–19, Legendary 20.
- No skill rust.
- `SkillLevelUp { id, skill, level }` fires at every gained level; 15 gives a happy thought at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20; reaching 20 emits `LegendaryReached`.

### Needs

Rates per hour (applied at the hourly boundary, scaled by Endurance for hunger and awake rest only: `rate × (150 − Endurance) / 100`):

| Need | Rise per hour | Relieved by | Want threshold | Critical threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hunger | 1 | eating: meal −100, raw food −70 | 50 | 85 |
| thirst | 1.6 (× `weather.thirstModifier()`) | drink: alcohol −100, water −80, a cactus −40 | 50 | 85 |
| rest | 5 awake; −15 × `rest` / 100 per hour on the sleep errand, from setting off for bed (§ Sleeping: −15 on a bed of 100) | sleeping | 60 | 90 |
| social | 2 | talking: −40 per conversation | 50 | none |
| comfort | 1 | sitting in a room of tier ≥ decent −40 per sit; hearth hall −20; a meal at a table is a sit | 55 | none |
| purpose | 2 while idle; −10 per hour of work | working | 60 | none |
| joy | 0.75 | hearth hall visit −30, which eating, drinking and sitting in the hall add up to; mist −30; fine meal −20; seeing a masterwork −10 | 60 | none |

The purpose rates, the critical thresholds and joy's relief from mist, a fine meal and a masterwork are *unmeasured*; the other rates, reliefs and want thresholds are measured as need errands per dwarf-day and shares of a dwarf's time ([balance model](../03-content/balance.md#needs-and-consumption)).

- A need at or above its critical threshold interrupts any job (11 returns the job to open with progress kept) and takes precedence in behavior selection. Hunger or thirst at `NEED_MAX` for `STARVE_DAYS` (hunger) or `DEHYDRATE_DAYS` (thirst) causes daily `Starvation { id }` damage through 17; at `NEED_MAX` one `Starving { id, need }` announcement (alert) fires per dwarf per day, naming hunger when both hunger and thirst are maxed; the log makes a day's into one line that names them all (24 § Announcements and the log). Daily starvation requests fire once if either duration has elapsed.
- Replacing a need activity with a weeping sulk cancels the activity before changing the brain: drop a held meal at the dwarf's current tile, release only its personal food or drink reservation through the item API with `ItemReleased`, delete the plan and stop its obsolete walk. A later selection may reserve an item anew; it must not orphan the old plan's reservation. A reservation that has passed to another holder is preserved.
- Threshold crossings and the sleep schedule set saved `interruptFlags` for the next behavior selection; a critical crossing emits `NeedCritical`, which 11 consumes to return an interruptible job to open with progress kept. A job held while the dwarf is away from work (a need, flight, shelter, a patient's bed; in practice one that is not interruptible) stays with the dwarf and resumes on its return, until 11 gives it back after `JOB_HOLD_TICKS` of absence.
- Rest at `NEED_MAX` makes the dwarf fall asleep where they stand ("passed out").
- Each need at or above its want threshold produces a standing negative thought while it persists (15: `hungry`, `thirsty`, `tired`, `lonely`, `uncomfortable`, `restless`, `bored`); satisfying it produces the positive event thought. An event thought lasts its row's hours: one that decays by `halflife` keeps at least ±1 (its whole value when smaller) until they end, and only then fades out under 1 (15 § Thoughts, Decay; T-569).

A food or drink errand reserves one unit under the dwarf's id. Death, unconsciousness, flight, a failed walk or the item becoming inedible or undrinkable abandons that errand. Release its still-owned reservation through the item API and emit one `ItemReleased { itemId, jobId: dwarfId }`; a held meal drops at the dwarf's tile. Never release another holder's reservation. Consumption ends the reservation by consuming the reserved unit.

### Eating and drinking

- Food search: nearest reachable edible item that is not forbidden or reserved, preferring a serving of a feast (§ Feasts), then `meal` items, then raw food, using 13's `findItem`. The dwarf carries it to the nearest dining hall chair with a table if one is within `DINING_SEARCH_RADIUS`, else eats on the spot. Emits `MealEaten { id, quality, atTable, roomTier }`, with `candlelit: true` for a meal at a table whose seat is in comfort light (14's `comfortLightAt`: the hearth's square, a lamp, a burning candle stand, a lantern), for 15's `ate_by_candlelight`; `feast: true` for a feast's serving, for 15's `feasted`; and `mug: true` for a meal at a seat whose table holds a mug for it (14's `seatHasMug`, 14 § Mugs at the table), for 15's `drank_from_a_mug`.
- Drink search: alcohol in a barrel on a stockpile, else alcohol in a barrel standing anywhere else (at the still, on a full fortress's floor), else a well with water, else the nearest reachable water tile of level ≥ 1 (not frozen) within `DRINK_SEARCH_RADIUS` tiles horizontally of the dwarf and 8 levels up or down, drunk from any walkable tile below `FLUID_WADE_LEVEL` 8-adjacent to it on its level, or on the level above it when open air with no fluid lies over the water (the bank over a brook or river, whose water lies one level below the ground, `local-map-generation.md` § 2; a floor or rock over the water is no bank). A brook that has frozen (04 § Freezing and thawing) gives no water until it thaws: a fortress with no drink in stock, no brew and no well on unfrozen water has nothing to drink through the winter. Rank the stand tiles by their horizontal Chebyshev distance from the dwarf plus their vertical distance times `Z_DISTANCE_WEIGHT`, ties by the stand tile index. If none of the listed sources is reachable, a reachable alcohol item in no barrel is a last resort; consume one unit and emit the same `DrankAlcohol` event. Emits `DrankAlcohol { id, material, quality, group, aged }` (`group` is the drink's group, `ale`, `wine` or `spirits`, from its material's tag; `aged` its stage, 0 young, 1 aged, 2 vintage, 13 § Aging in a cellar) or `DrankWater { id }` (dwarves prefer ale; water gives a mild negative thought). 15 gives `drank_strong_drink` for spirits, and `drank_aged_drink` or `drank_vintage_drink` beside the drink's own thought.
- After every drink source above, the alcohol in no barrel included, a thirsty dwarf takes a reachable `plant_food` whose material is tagged `drinkable` (`cactus_pulp`), walks to it and eats it there for its water: thirst falls by `CACTUS_THIRST_RELIEF` (half of water's), hunger is not relieved. Emits `DrankFromPlant { id, material }`, and 15 gives `drank_cactus`.
- Eating and drinking take `EAT_TICKS` and `DRINK_TICKS`, longer on a crowded tile (22 § Crowding).
- **Tallow smoke.** On each `HourPassed`, after the needs, while a candle stand is built, each fortress dwarf standing on a tile lit only by stands burning tallow (14's `tallowSmokeAt`) draws `TALLOW_SMOKE_PERMILLE` (14) on this system's stream; on a hit it emits `SmokyFuelBurned { workshopId: 0, tile, dwarfId }`, and 15 gives that dwarf `smoky_hall` (14 § Candle stands and lanterns). No other dwarf draws.
- A meal eaten at a table is also a sit in the room of its chair. It relieves comfort as a sit there would and emits `Sat { id, roomTier }`: −40 in a room of tier ≥ decent, −20 in the hearth hall, nothing at a table in neither.
- A meal or a drink finished on a hearth-hall tile counts its `EAT_TICKS` or `DRINK_TICKS` toward a hearth visit (§ Social, comfort, joy).
- Patients are fed by care jobs (17).

### Feasts

- A `feast` (`food/meal/feast`, `items.md` § Food) is a stack of 20 servings that rots in a day, cooked at the kitchen (12 § Feasts). It is food, hauled and served as meals are, with no placement of its own: the food search takes a serving of a feast before any other meal, so a feast in the dining hall's stockpile feeds the hall for its day.
- A dwarf who eats a serving emits `MealEaten` with `feast: true` and thinks `feasted` (15). The feast's `ItemCrafted` gives everyone `held_a_feast` (15), the cook and those who do not eat it too: midwinter has a reason.

### Tipsy

- A dwarf who drinks a drink of the `drink/spirits` group is tipsy for `TIPSY_TICKS`: its walking speed and its work rate are × (100 − `TIPSY_SLOW_PERCENT`) / 100, and a talk relieves its social need twice over (−80, a friend −120; the partner's relief is its own). `dwarves.productivity(id)` includes it, and the movement formula of § Movement gains the factor. A second drink while tipsy restarts the time; it does not stack. A tipsy time that has run out, or whose dwarf has died, is forgotten on the dwarves tick.
- Dwarves drink the nearest drink as now, so where the spirits stand is where the singing is: a barrel of spirits by the forge is a slow forge, a locked cellar a wine cellar.
- The units panel and the inspector say "Tipsy" beside the activity; the glyph is unchanged.

### Sleeping

- Schedule: each dwarf has `sleepStartHour` (uniformly 20–23 from 10's rng stream) and goes to bed at that hour unless rest is below 20. Rest at its want threshold outside the window also sends the dwarf to bed. Rest falls at the sleeping rate from the moment the dwarf sets off for its bed (or the floor it will sleep on), the walk included: the walk to bed is part of resting. A sleep ends when rest reaches 0, or after `SLEEP_HOURS` × 100 / `rest` hours if that comes first, and the dwarf wakes with rest 0, so a dwarf whose walk to bed is long can reach it rested, lie down and get up at once.
- **Beds rest differently.** Every building tagged `sleep` has a `rest` percent (`buildings-and-furniture.md`; 14 § Beds): `pallet` and `straw_pallet` 90, `stone_bed` 95, `bed` 100, and a made-up bed `MADE_UP_REST_PERCENT` (110). Sleeping on the floor rests at `FLOOR_REST_PERCENT` (85), below every bed. On the sleep errand rest falls by `15 × rest / 100` an hour (§ Needs), read live from the bed the dwarf holds (`buildings.restPercent`) or the floor. So a dwarf on the floor sleeps about a sixth longer than one in a wooden bed, one on a pallet a ninth longer, one on a stone bed a twentieth, and one in a made-up bed about a tenth less. The band is narrow on purpose (T-572): what a bed mostly gives is a thought (15), the floor −2, a pallet −1, a stone bed none, a wooden bed +1, a dressed bed +3 and a featherbed +5, so a bed is worth making without a fortress that has none falling behind.
- Bed search: own bed (assigned bedroom), else any unassigned free bed, else the floor of the hearth hall, else where they stand. A pallet and a stone bed are beds. Emits `Slept { id, bedQuality, roomTier, onFloor, outdoors, ownBed, restored, rest, bedding, bedType }`: `rest` the percent slept at, `bedding` what the bed was made up with (`none`, `soft`, `feather`: 14's `buildings.bedding`), `bedType` the bed's building type ('' on the floor). 15's thoughts: `slept_on_floor` on the floor, `slept_on_a_pallet` for a pallet, `slept_in_a_bed` for a wooden bed that is not dressed, `slept_in_a_soft_bed` in its place for a bed made up with bedding and a pillow, `slept_in_a_featherbed` in place of the soft one for a featherbed; a bed with bedding and no pillow rests at 110 and gives `slept_in_a_bed`, and a stone bed gives none.
- A built bed holds one dwarf. Choosing it for sleep holds it immediately, during the walk and sleep, until the dwarf wakes, abandons the activity or dies. The saved rest activity's `itemId` is the bed id (0 for floor sleep); loading preserves the hold. Bed availability is live, not cached with room geometry. An own bed held by another dwarf is unavailable too; another free unassigned bed or the floor is the fallback. A patient holds its assigned bed under 17's rule, so a tired medic never chooses it.
- Sleep is interrupted by `danger`-severity events nearby and by hearth alert.
- A sleeper whose hunger or thirst is the critical need furthest over its mark (§ Behavior selection's ranking) wakes at the hour: the sleep is abandoned with its rest kept, and the dwarf eats or drinks first. A sleep lasts up to `SLEEP_HOURS` × 100 / `rest` hours, eight on the floor, and without this a dwarf that lay down hungry would spend them at a critical hunger.
- A soldier on duty who walks to a bed or sleeps is serving a need: in every squad mode, 16's squad code neither moves it nor gives it a target until it is back on duty (16 § Squads).

### Social, comfort, joy

- A talk needs no trip. Every `TALK_CHECK_TICKS` each awake dwarf that is not walking and whose social need is at or over its want threshold looks, through the spatial index, for another awake dwarf that is not walking within Chebyshev distance 2 on the same level; a friend first, then the lowest id. The two start a talk in passing. Awake means conscious and not asleep; a dwarf is walking while it has a walk under way. A dwarf already in a talk, or on a social errand, is not chosen. Two dwarves eating at the same table talk this way.
- A talk in passing lasts `TALK_TICKS`. It is checked every `TALK_CHECK_TICKS`: if either dwarf has died, fallen asleep or unconscious, started to walk, or is no longer within distance 2, the talk ends with no effect. Otherwise at its end both get `Talked { a, b }` and its relief, exactly as for a social errand. Neither dwarf is held at its work. A dwarf that finishes a meal, a drink, a sit or a hearth visit during the talk stays where it is until the talk ends, and so does a dwarf with no work; a threat, an alert or a critical need takes it away as from any activity.
- Sparring is company. Each session of a sparring pair at the barracks (16 § Training, `TrainingSession` with two members) is a talk between the two: both get `Talked` and its relief, and it counts toward their friendship, exactly as a talk in passing. It needs no want: a soldier at drill keeps its social need low, and a squad that drills together becomes friends.
- Social errand: go to the nearest reachable hearth-hall or meeting-area tile and wait up to `SOCIAL_WAIT_TICKS`; the first living dwarf within Chebyshev distance 2 on the same level who is not asleep (friends first, then by id) is the partner; both get `Talked { a, b }` after `TALK_TICKS`; relationships increment; friends after `FRIEND_TALKS` talks. Talking to a friend relieves 60 instead of 40. The partner is not interrupted.
- Comfort errand: sit on a free chair in the best-tier room within reach for `SIT_TICKS`; without a chair in a room of tier ≥ decent, sit in the hearth hall, on its stand tile (§ Behavior selection, step 9).
- Joy errand: stand in the hearth hall, on its stand tile (§ Behavior selection, step 9), for `HEARTH_VISIT_TICKS`; this is a hearth visit (`HearthVisited`, −30).
- Time in the hearth hall is a hearth visit. Each meal, drink or sit a dwarf finishes on a hearth-hall tile adds its `EAT_TICKS`, `DRINK_TICKS` or `SIT_TICKS` to the dwarf's saved count. When the count reaches `HEARTH_VISIT_TICKS` the dwarf gets one hearth visit (`HearthVisited`, −30) and the count returns to zero.
- Joy is also relieved by mist (`DwarfFeltMist`), warm springs (`DwarfNearSpring`), fine meals and seeing a masterwork item in a room they enter (`SawMasterwork`, once per item per dwarf). Room entry inspects floor items and installed furniture throughout the entered room, excluding container contents and the viewer's own work. Save the per-dwarf seen-item memory across visits and loads.
- The three errands are taken only at step 8 of behavior selection, by a dwarf with no work.
- Hall errand: when social, comfort or joy is at or over `LEISURE_MARK`, a dwarf takes one sit in the hearth hall before ordinary work (step 6). It walks to the nearest free chair on a hearth-hall tile, or to its stand tile (§ Behavior selection, step 9) when no chair is free, and sits for `SIT_TICKS`. The sit relieves comfort as any sit there does and its `SIT_TICKS` count toward a hearth visit, which they complete. It is also the talk: every `TALK_CHECK_TICKS` while seated, a dwarf with social at or over its want threshold and no talk in passing under way takes as partner any other dwarf within Chebyshev distance 2 on its level that is not asleep (a friend first, then the lowest id), as on a social errand; at the end of the sit both get `Talked` and its relief. The partner is not interrupted. Without a hearth hall there is no hall errand.
- If a hall errand ends with one of the three still at or over `LEISURE_MARK` and no talk under way (nobody came within reach), the dwarf takes no other hall errand for `LEISURE_RETRY_TICKS`. The tick is saved per dwarf.
- Idle dwarves without a want go to the hearth hall and stand or sit there; the hearth hall is the default place.

### Behavior selection

Evaluated when the dwarf has nothing to do, when a need crosses a threshold, and when interrupted by an event. `PatientAdmitted` and `PatientDischarged` set its interrupt flag. Priority order, the first that applies wins:

1. `flee`: 16 has flagged the dwarf as threatened (a hostile within `THREAT_RADIUS` and the dwarf is not a soldier on duty): path away from the threat toward the hearth hall. After fleeing, remember the hostile and its last seen tile while it lives and remains within `THREAT_RADIUS` of that tile. Changing level or reaching the hall does not clear this memory. Do not start a noncritical need or a job whose target is on that tile's level within horizontal Chebyshev `THREAT_RADIUS`; serve a safe reachable target instead, or wait in the hearth hall. Critical needs override this avoidance and continue across threat scans, except under attack: a dwarf whose held hostile is within `THREAT_RADIUS` and holds it as its combat target (16 § Targeting) flees, or fights when cornered, whatever its needs, and a need activity in progress ends at the threat scan. So a patient never rests (step 3) within the reach of a hostile that attacks it. A civilian sheltering under the `hearth` alert (step 2) whose shelter a hostile party member has entered, and whom that member attacks, does not flee inside the shelter: with both inside the shelter area of step 2, it fights back as a cornered civilian does (below), with the weapon or tool it holds or else bare-handed. A civilian outside its shelter still flees, and soldiers are unchanged.

Fire is checked before step 1, every tick, for every conscious dwarf, a soldier on duty included. A dwarf is in danger when it stands on a burning tile, or on a tile that can burn (05 § Fire) while a cardinal neighbour or the tile below it burns. It drops what it is doing (an activity is cancelled; a job returns to the board with its progress kept, and an item it carries is dropped where it stands) and walks to the nearest tile it reaches that is not burning and cannot burn: breadth first by legal step count (burning tiles block movement) within `FIRE_FLEE_NODE_LIMIT` tiles, ties by tile index. With none in reach, it walks to the nearest tile in no danger even if that tile can burn, and flees again when the fire reaches it. Its activity reads "Fleeing the fire" and its behavior is `flee` with no threat held. On arrival, or once it stands on safe ground, it selects again. This holds for a sleeper, a conscious patient and a dwarf in a critical need; it takes precedence over every step below. An unconscious dwarf does not move. With no tile in reach that is out of danger the dwarf stays where it is. A dwarf remembers its last tick in danger from fire or running from it, in its brain's saved state (absent before its first flight and in older saves). For `FIRE_AVOID_TICKS` ticks after that tick it starts no want-level need activity whose target lies within `FIRE_AVOID_RADIUS` tiles (horizontal Chebyshev, same level) of a burning tile, and 11 offers it no job whose tile lies that near one, so it does not walk back to the fire's edge and flee again; a critical need overrides this, as it overrides the avoidance of a remembered hostile (step 1).

Rising water is checked in the same place, for the same dwarves, by the same flight, asleep and talking included. A dwarf is in danger on a tile of unfrozen water at `FLUID_DEEP_LEVEL` or more, or at `FLUID_WADE_LEVEL` when it has stopped there; one still walking through wading water is on its way out of it. It drops what it is doing as for fire and walks, activity text "Leaving the water", to the nearest tile with no water it reaches by legal steps, breadth first within `WATER_FLEE_NODE_LIMIT` tiles, ties by tile index; with none, the nearest tile below `FLUID_WADE_LEVEL`. With neither, because all its neighbours are already deep or walled, it stays and drowns (17). It selects again on arrival.

Dread is checked next, in the same place, for the same dwarves. A dwarf under an unexpired `flee` effect (25 § Effects: a dread hexer's Dread, or a fearsome blow) drops what it is doing as for fire (an activity is cancelled, its combat target is cleared, a job returns to the board with its progress kept, and an item it carries is dropped where it stands) and runs by 16's `stepFled`: toward the tile `FLEE_EFFECT_TILES` from the effect's source through itself, and it stops there. Its activity reads "Fleeing in dread" and its behavior is `flee` with no threat held, so its squad does not move it or give it a target (16 § Squads) and 16 hits it as a fleeing creature. A soldier on duty runs too. A fire or rising water under it takes precedence, and the dread flight resumes after. When the effect ends it stops and selects again: a soldier on duty goes back to its squad, a civilian to its work.
2. `retreat`: fortress alert is `hearth` and the dwarf is a civilian: path to the hearth hall, or the nearest indoor tile behind a door (never the door's own tile) if the hall is unreachable, the tile 19 fixed for the dwarf when the alert started; stay until the alert ends; a sheltering dwarf that is unconscious when the alert falls leaves `retreat` at once and chooses afresh on waking. A critical need overrides the retreat, as it overrides `flee`: a sheltering civilian whose hunger, thirst or rest has reached its critical threshold goes to serve it by step 4's rules, wherever the food, drink or bed is, and returns to shelter when the need is served while the alert lasts. Medical work does the same: a sheltering civilian that holds a job for a patient (17: the jobs `carry_patient`, `feed_patient`, `water_patient` and the treatments) keeps it, and one that can claim such a job takes it, so long as the job is open to it by 11's rules (a remembered hostile near the patient excludes it, and `flee` ends a job in progress, which returns to the board); it works the job outside the shelter and returns to shelter when the job ends while the alert lasts. A lever does the same: a sheltering civilian that holds or can claim the `pull_lever` job of a lever inside its shelter (11) works it and returns to shelter. Under the alert 11 offers a sheltering civilian patient-care jobs, those pulls, and the jobs done wholly inside its shelter (farming, cooking, brewing and hauling within it, 11 § Assignment), so that a civilian sheltered for weeks keeps the shelter's food, drink and goods going; nothing that takes it outside the shelter is offered. A want-level need does not end the retreat, except that a sheltering civilian serves a want-level hunger, thirst or rest from a target inside the shelter (the food, the drink, or a bed, for rest, in the hearth hall, or in the indoor area it shelters in when there is no hall: the tiles around its retreat tile that are not `outdoor` and are reached along the walker graph (a level changes only by stairs or a ramp) without passing a door, hatch, floodgate or drawbridge), which does not expose it; with no such target it stays. Social, comfort and joy are not served under the alert. A civilian for whom no shelter is reachable (19 `retreatTile` gives none: there is no hearth hall, no indoor tile behind a door and no bedroom, or it is shut out by a locked gate) skips this step until one becomes reachable, and works as under a `watchful` alert: 11 offers it every job it would be offered then, and it serves its needs and leisure as usual, so the alert does not leave it standing in the open with nothing to do; `flee` and the remembered-threat avoidance of step 1 still protect it. When a shelter becomes reachable (the watched ids are rechecked, 19 § Hearth alert retreat), it is interrupted, gives back a job outside the shelter as at the alert's start (11 § Assignment), and retreats. Under the hearth alert an interrupt (a need crossing its mark, an alert or shelter change) cancels what the dwarf is doing and selects afresh, except an errand to eat or drink at a critical need, which goes on: restarting it at every interrupt walked the dwarf back and forth between the hall and the food.
3. `patient`: 17 has marked the dwarf as needing a hospital: go to the hospital bed (or the nearest bed) and rest; care jobs feed them. A patient that 17 bandages where it lies (a fresh wound bleeding without a bandage, 17 § Patients and hospitals) first rests on its current tile, as a patient without a bed does, and walks to the bed when 17's wait ends. A conscious patient does this under any alert, a `hearth` alert included (step 2 does not hold it in shelter); an unconscious one is carried. A soldier on duty is the exception while a hostile is in sight (step 5's definition): it stays on duty and fights on, and takes `patient` on the first think after the last hostile in sight is dead or gone, in every squad mode and under every alert. It leaves its squad's orders with that step (16 § Squads) and returns to duty on discharge while it is still on duty. Its want-level needs and scheduled sleep wait during that fight in every mode; a critical need still takes step 4. When the fight ends, patient rest takes precedence even over an ongoing need activity: abandon the activity, release its held food or drink through the usual cancellation path, and walk to the bed.
4. `need` (critical): satisfy the most critical need. Always applies to soldiers on duty as well as civilians, in every squad mode.
5. `duty`: the dwarf is a soldier on duty (16): 16 controls movement and targets. A soldier on duty works no job: one it held when it was called to duty is interrupted with its progress kept and returns to the board for another worker (11 § Job steps). Apart from the fighting patient of step 3, duty holds the soldier against its want-level needs (step 6) and its scheduled sleep in two cases only: its squad is treated as active (`active` mode, or any mode under a `watchful` or `hearth` alert, 16 § Squads) and a hostile is in sight; or its squad has an explicit order (`station`, `patrol`, `follow`, `kill`) during a `watchful` or `hearth` alert. A hostile is in sight when it is alive, not stealthed, within `PERCEPTION_RADIUS` (16) horizontally and two levels vertically, and in line of sight; a combat target the soldier still holds is not by itself in sight, since 16 keeps a target that is out of sight (16 § Targeting). In every other case the want takes precedence: training, a station, patrol, follow or kill order while the alert is `calm`, and an alert without an order do not hold it, and the soldier returns to duty when the need is met. A critical need (step 4) wins in every case.
6. `need` (want): satisfy the highest of hunger, thirst and rest over its want threshold, unless a job with priority ≤ `NEED_DEFER_PRIORITY` is available and the need is below its critical threshold. Social, comfort and joy are not served here below `LEISURE_MARK`: they wait for step 8 and are relieved in passing (§ Eating and drinking; § Social, comfort, joy). With none of hunger, thirst and rest to serve, a dwarf with one of the three at or over `LEISURE_MARK` takes the hall errand here, before default-priority work and under the same exception for a job with priority ≤ `NEED_DEFER_PRIORITY`. A soldier whose duty does not hold (step 5) never reaches step 8, so it serves the highest of all its wants here, those three included.
7. `work`: queue the dwarf for 11 through `jobs.assign(id)` and stay in `work` while waiting for its later tick to answer. With a job, run its steps; when no longer queued and without a job, go idle.
8. `social`: the dwarf has no work: 11 has answered with no job and reports no open job the dwarf could take, or the dwarf cannot or may not work (a day off, a wound, a child with no hauling to do). A dwarf in a talk in passing stays for it. Otherwise do the errand of the highest of social, comfort and joy over its want threshold. No such errand starts while a job is open for the dwarf; only the hall errand of step 6 does.
9. `idle`: go to the hearth hall and wait up to `IDLE_TICKS`, then re-evaluate; re-evaluate at once if a job becomes available for this dwarf before the wait is over, so that new work is never ignored for a whole hour. Idle dwarves read "No job" as their activity text, which the panels show as "Idle" (§ Player-facing behavior). An idle dwarf stands on its *stand tile*, a hall tile of its own, as does a dwarf on a joy errand, or on a comfort or hall errand with no chair to sit on (§ Social, comfort, joy): it keeps the hall tile it stands on, if no building stands there (a door, the hearth, a chair), while no other creature stands there (a creature walking through does not count, and a dwarf of a higher id does not move it); otherwise it takes the hall tile it can reach and stand on, with no building on it, that has the fewest creatures on it or heading to it (an idle dwarf or one on an errand heads to its goal, any other creature to the end of its walk), ties by the nearest to the hearth (horizontal Chebyshev distance; to the dwarf itself when the hall holds no built hearth), then the lowest index, and moves only if that tile has fewer than its own. So the dwarves who come in by one way gather round the hearth, each on a tile of its own, instead of all standing on the hall's first tile, where they would crowd everyone passing (22 § Crowding), and they leave a tile a tame animal stands on, since passers-by do not move the animal (08 § Tame behavior).

`DwarfIdle` fires once per stretch without a job and is reset by real work. With no hearth hall, idle uses the saved arrival wagon tile; without an arrival it stays at the current tile. Selection repairs legacy negative goals before queued work. Patient rest without a bed, cornered fighting and duty also use the current tile. A patient resting without a bed never rests on a door's tile, where a door will not lock on it (14): it rests on the lowest-index tile beside the door, on the same level, that it can stand on, that holds no door and that shares the door tile's walker label, and stays on the door only when there is none.

Civilian escape searches the legal walker edges, including stairs and ramps, with the dwarf's movement restrictions. Visit at most `CIVILIAN_FLEE_NODE_LIMIT` tiles breadth first by step count, breaking ties within each frontier by tile index. Prefer a reachable safer hearth-hall tile even when the bounded search cannot visit it: use the hall tile reached by the search, or the lowest-index eligible hall tile when none was visited. Only without an eligible reachable hall use the nearest visited safer tile. A safer tile is on another level from the threat or has greater horizontal Chebyshev distance from it than the starting tile. Exclude hostile-occupied tiles. If no safer tile is found, the civilian is cornered and fights back with the weapon or tool they carry: the weapon it wields, else the tool it holds, when 16 counts that item as a weapon (16 § Equipment), else its natural attack. A load it hauls is not a weapon.

A civilian is *caught* when, while fleeing, it is hit in melee (from an adjacent tile on its level) by a wild animal that is hunting it, or by a cavern monster (18), whose movement speed (`Motion.speed`) is at least its own: running is no escape from a creature that does not let go. An animal hunts when its state is `hunt`, or `attack` with no defended tile (08 § Wild behavior). A squad member off duty is caught by any hit in melee from any attacker, at any speed, whether or not it was fleeing, and fights with the gear it carries. For any other civilian, a hit from an animal defending its ground (`attack` with a defended tile), or from a hostile of a party (a thief, a bandit, a raider, the restless dead, the Deep), does not catch it: it keeps fleeing, and a defending animal lets it go once it is out of the animal's provoke radius from where the attack began. From then on it is cornered by that attacker and fights it, by the rules of a cornered fight below, and its escape search admits only a tile it reaches through a tile the attacker cannot enter (a door that the attacker cannot pass): the first such tile in the same breadth-first order, with no hall preference and no distance test; none found means it stays and fights. It stops being caught when its attacker no longer holds it as its combat target (the attacker broke off or fled), when the attacker dies, or when it takes such a route; it then selects normally, and flees again if the attacker is still near. A threat scan from another hostile does not replace a caught civilian's attacker. A civilian who fights back, cornered or caught, does not chase: it strikes only an enemy within melee reach (16 § Targeting). The caught mark is saved with the held threat; older saves have none.

A critical need always interrupts a civilian's cornered fight, even when no resource can be found, unless the enemy attacks it (step 1). An initial escape has first priority; after fleeing, a critical need may return through the remembered danger once nothing attacks the dwarf. Repeated threat scans do not restart a critical need activity, including when an escape is available; a newly more critical need still interrupts it normally. A cornered civilian with no enemy in melee reach and no melee or ranged attack opportunity for `CIVILIAN_COMBAT_TIMEOUT_TICKS` leaves combat, clears its held threat and combat target, stops its combat walk, and selects normally. Melee reach or an attack opportunity refreshes the deadline; another threat scan does not. Save that deadline in `Brain.behaviorUntil` and the held threat in the system state. Omit the threat list when empty; older saves without it start with no held threats, and older records without a tile capture the saved hostile position on load. Leaving a fight clears its activity caption before normal selection queues work. A later threat scan may acquire a new threat.

Only shut-out civilians are watched for shelter becoming reachable. Recheck those ids on `AlertChanged`, `BuildingStateChanged` or a changed `pathing.connectivityRevision`, preserving flee precedence; save the watched ids. The revision is derived and does not change connectivity labels or saved path state.

Children skip 5 and 7 except for hauling jobs (they have only the `hauling` labor and it cannot be turned off).

Every target a need activity walks to must be reachable (connectivity labels). If the walk still fails, the dwarf does not retry that need for `NEED_RETRY_TICKS`.

### Movement

Dwarves move with the movement helper at `speedFixed = species speed × (50 + Agility) / 100 × wound modifier (17) × tipsy modifier (§ Tipsy) × carry modifier (a carried item heavier than `HEAVY_ITEM_DENSITY × size` halves speed)`. They avoid `restricted` tiles when a path without them exists (22 cost) and never enter `forbidden` tiles. A dwarf entering a misty tile emits `DwarfFeltMist` (once per `MIST_THOUGHT_COOLDOWN`); entering an outdoor tile during rain, storm, snow, heatwave or cold snap emits `DwarfOutdoorsIn { weather }` once per hour.

The `EntitiesMoved` handler observes entries using the batch’s from/to tiles. It writes the dwarf’s `Brain` for mist and outdoor thought memory, and `Needs` for masterwork joy relief; it never changes a tile. A zero `lastMistTick` means mist has never been felt; otherwise the cooldown must have elapsed. `lastOutdoorThoughtHour` stores the hour index so the weather thought fires only once per hour. `DwarfNearSpring` fires when the destination is on or 8-adjacent to a spring on the same level and the origin was not. `CraterEntered { id, first }` fires when the destination is one of a `starfall` crater's tiles and the origin was not, checked against 03's sorted `craterTiles` (`terrain.isCrater`), as a spring is; `first` is true for the fortress's first such entry (the saved flag `craterEntered`), whose note is "{dwarf} stood where the star fell." (`announce.crater_entered`); 15's `saw_the_star` keeps itself from repeating while it lasts. `RibsEntered { id, boneyard }` fires when the destination is one of a boneyard's cage tiles (03 § Local map generation, stage 9b), checked against 03's sorted `boneyards` (`terrain.boneyardAt`), and no fortress dwarf has stood in that boneyard's ribs (`terrain.boneyardEntered`): once a boneyard, the first dwarf there. Two dwarves entering one boneyard in the same batch make one event; 03 marks its record (`boneyardsEntered`) when the event is delivered, before the next batch. Its note is "{dwarf} stood inside the ribs of something enormous." (`announce.ribs_entered`), and 15 gives the dwarf `stood_in_the_ribs`. Miasma is a standing thought checked by 15, rather than a movement event.

### Relationships

`Relationships.friends` and `grudges`; `talkCount: Map<id, n>`. Friend at `FRIEND_TALKS` talks; a sulk argument (15) adds a grudge; grudges fade after `GRUDGE_DAYS`. Friends' deaths cause stronger grief (15). Dwarves choose friends preferentially to talk to.

### Titles

- `AssignTitle { dwarfId, title }` and `AssignTitle { dwarfId: 0, title }` to vacate. One dwarf per title; a dwarf may hold several. The saved per-role holder record is authoritative; reassigning or vacating one role preserves other roles. The singular `Title` component caches the first held role in title-table order, while `dwarves.titles(id)` returns them all.
- Thane: the fortress-wide thought `thane_hall_grand` or `thane_hall_bare` depending on the Thane's bedroom tier (15, recomputed daily); the Thane's presence in the hearth hall lets talking relieve `THANE_SOCIAL_BONUS` more; and once a season the Thane has a whim (§ The Thane's whim).
- Warden: squads gain `WARDEN_TRAINING_BONUS_PERCENT` training experience; the Warden is the default squad leader.
- Broker: their `trading` skill is used at the depot; without a Broker any dwarf with the `trading` labor trades at skill 0.
- Chief Medic: their `medicine` skill is used for diagnosis quality; care and treatment use the treating dwarf's skill.

### The Thane's whim

- On each `SeasonChanged` this system first judges the whim of the season that ended, if there is one, then, if the fortress has a living Thane, draws the next.
- **The draw.** One whim from `whims` (`personality-and-thoughts.md` § Whims), uniformly among the rows that are *possible*, on a stream of its own (`10.whims`, so a fortress with no Thane draws the same numbers everywhere else, and nothing is drawn while no row is possible). A row is possible when its item type is in the content and one such item, of its material and quality, is in stock (available), or a built workshop (its magma variant counts) has a recipe that makes that type: of the row's material when it names one, by a recipe that fixes its output's material to it, or makes it from an input of which an item of that material is in stock; and the recipe's inputs are *at hand*. A row of `any` item is possible with any built workshop's quality recipe whose inputs are at hand. The Thane wishes for what the fortress could make from what it has, not for what its map and its caravans have never given it: no mead without honey, no lantern without glass or sand. Emits `WhimSet { thaneId, whimId }`, a note that says how far the fortress already is (the whim's *progress*, as the judgement counts it): "Ketil Ironvein, Thane, wishes for fine meals for everyone by the end of the season: 3 of 20 fine meals in stock." or "…wishes for a lantern in the dining hall by the end of the season: 0 of 1 in place." (`announce.thane_whim`). The whim is saved (`whim`: its row and the tick it was set).
  - **At hand.** An input is at hand when its `count` units that answer it (its match, material and material property; of one material when it asks for the same) are in stock (available), or when a built workshop has a recipe whose output answers it, of a material the input takes, and whose own inputs are in stock: a recipe that fixes its output's material answers when that material is one the input takes, and one whose output takes its material from an input answers when that input is in stock of a material the first input takes (a mason's blocks are of the stone cut, never a lantern's glass); a brew's drink is taken as the plant's ale or wine. One step only: sand and tallow are at hand for a lantern where a kiln and a craftsdwarf's workshop stand, honey for spirits where a still stands (mead, then deepfire), but nothing two steps away. Fuel is not counted.
  - **Quality.** A row that names a quality (the masterwork) takes a quality recipe and a fortress dwarf whose level in the recipe's skill could roll it: its highest roll, the level × 5 + 40 (12 § Quality), reaches the tier. A masterwork is possible from level 13.
  - **Stuffing.** A room row made up with a stuffing (the featherbed's feathers) is possible only when each item its building wants (a bedding, a pillow; 14 § Beds) is at hand stuffed with it: one in stock with that stuffing, or a built workshop's recipe that makes it from inputs at hand, its last input (the stuffing, 12 § Operation) of that material. A built building of the row's type counts as its building.
  - The draw reads the stock and the workshops as they stand at the `SeasonChanged`: honey bought in the season after is no reason for that season's whim.
- **The judgement.** On the next `SeasonChanged`, before the next draw: a `stock` whim is met when at least `count` units of its item type, of its material and quality when it names them, are in stock (available: not forbidden, reserved, owned or installed); a `room` whim when `count` built buildings of its type, of its material when it names one and made up with feathers when it says so (14's `bedding`), stand in a zone of its zone type (the Thane's own when it says so). Emits `WhimJudged { thaneId, whimId, met }` (`thaneId` the living Thane then, 0 for none), a note: "The Thane's wish was met: a lantern in the dining hall." (`announce.thane_whim_met`) or "The Thane wished for a lantern in the dining hall, and did not get it." (`announce.thane_whim_missed`). Met, everyone thinks `thane_pleased`; missed, the Thane alone thinks `thane_grumbled`, and nobody else anything (15).
- **All face, no spiral.** A whim has no other effect: no other thought, no sulk, no mood cascade, nothing a job or a need reads (flavour report item 26). A whim whose row is gone from the content is judged missed. A Thane who dies or is replaced leaves the whim standing for the new one.
- `dwarves.whim()` gives the whim of the season, its text, its kind, its `count`, its progress now (`have`: the units in stock of a `stock` whim, the buildings in place of a `room` whim, counted as the judgement counts them) and whether it is met now, for the units panel's Thane line.

### Children and aging

- Age advances yearly on `YearPassed`. A child becomes an adult at `ADULT_AGE_YEARS` (`DwarfBecameAdult`; labors all enabled; a "came of age" thought for the family).
- No births in version 1. No death by age.

### Clothing

Dwarves wear a body garment, a leg garment and boots (items with the `clothing` tag in three slots of `Equipment`). Missing clothing gives the `unclothed` thought and doubles cold injury risk (17). Clothing does not wear out. Immigrants and settlers arrive with real carried cloth garments in the body, legs and feet slots, selecting each by clothing category and the material by cloth class from the registry. The `over` slot is optional; a missing required slot makes `isUnclothed` true. The same outfit helper serves both arrival paths; the clothier makes more for the naked.

### Death

17 emits `CreatureDied`; before removing the dwarf, this system calls 11's `jobs.workerDied` with the recorded death tile to release held, unworn tools and carried items. This system removes the dwarf from titles and squads (16 handles squads on the same event), updates population. All death consumers use this single event and its recorded species, faction, name, age and tile after the entity is removed. The corpse item is created by 13; burial is 14 and 17.

## Extension points

- Births: a pregnancy field on married pairs and a `DwarfBorn` event; children already exist.
- Skill rust: a decay pass in the daily handler.
- More needs (faith, beauty): a row in the needs table and an activity in behavior selection.
- Personality-driven job preferences: a bias term in 11's scoring that reads `Personality`.
