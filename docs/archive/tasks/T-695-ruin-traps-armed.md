# T-695 — A ruin's traps still stand, some still armed

**Closed:** 2026-11-24, in 31ef0886,c59c0f09.

**Source:** T-692 follow-up · **Size:** M · **Needs:** T-692 · **Status:** open

## Why

A ruin keeps its halls, some items, its furniture (T-690) and its workshops, hearth, depots and wells as wrecks (T-692, 02 § Ruins, 14 § Wrecks). Its mechanisms and traps are gone: the lever that raised the old drawbridge, the weapon traps in the old gate corridor. An old fortress's gate still set with a weapon trap or two that bite the new expedition's first miner (or the first goblin to walk in) is a story a re-embark could tell, and a fun one (`docs/fun-features.md`).

## What to do

Decide whether a ruin's traps stand (as wrecks that do nothing, or a few still armed, with their loaded weapons weathered), and its levers, plates, floodgates and bridges (wrecks, links lost). Record them beside the wrecks (`RuinRecord.wrecks`, or a trap list with the loaded items), place them at arrival through 14 (`placeWreck` or a sibling), and say in 19 whom an armed old trap catches (no faction owns it: everyone, the new dwarves too, until it is disarmed or taken apart). Keep the record small, the placement deterministic, and fresh embarks unchanged (world hashes). Spec it in 02 § Ruins, 14 § Wrecks and 19.

## Decisions

- **Mechanisms are wrecks, links lost.** Every built lever, pressure plate, floodgate and drawbridge (and such a wreck left standing) stands as a T-692 wreck holding every second unit: a lever, plate or floodgate holds nothing (its one unit is the first), a drawbridge its second block, its fourth … and its mechanism. A wreck takes no link and no pull (both ask for `built`), a floodgate's wreck holds no water back and a drawbridge's neither raises nor blocks: its deck lies passable on whatever tiles the ruin left (a lowered bridge's tiles are floor in the old map, so the moat under it is covered; a raised one's stay open). A drawbridge keeps its size in the record (`width`, `height`, unturned, only for a variable footprint), since a 1×1 stand-in would misplace it.
- **Traps: a wreck, or a few still armed.** A trap's wreck holds nothing: its mechanism is its first unit and the looters took its load. A weapon, cage or stone-fall trap that is `armed` with all it was loaded with stays armed with `RUIN_TRAP_ARMED_PERMILLE` (250, *unmeasured*: "a few"), its loaded items weathered a quality level, as a kept item is (`Item.wear` is read by nothing, so quality is the weathering). A spike trap fires only when a link extends it, and links are lost, so it is always a wreck; so is a spent or jammed trap.
- **The record stays small and in one list.** An armed trap is a `wrecks` entry with `loaded` (type, material, quality per item); nothing new in `RuinRecord`, and stage 10b, arrival and `placeWreck` carry it on the wreck path. Mechanisms and traps come after the workshops' wrecks, trimmed on their own to `RUIN_MECHANISMS_MAX` (20, *unmeasured*), so a fortress of a hundred levers does not push its forge out of the ruin, and the workshops' trim draws exactly what T-692 drew. Draw order: tiles, items, furniture, the wrecks' trim, each armable trap's chance in id order, the mechanisms' trim.
- **An armed old trap is built, armed and no one's: a new trap mode `anyone`.** 14 builds it at once with its loaded items made anew and installed, its one mechanism as its consumed input, mode `anyone`, and emits `BuildingCompleted`, so 19 registers it from its building as any trap. Under `anyone` it springs on every creature, the fortress's dwarves and tame animals too (`all` spares tame animals). The player cannot set `anyone` (`SetTrapMode` refuses it, `bad_setting`).
- **Disarming is choosing its mode, or taking it apart.** Choosing `enemies` or `all` makes it the fortress's: from then on an ordinary trap, sparing the dwarves under `enemies`. Instant, like any `SetTrapMode`: the cost is finding it first. A separate disarm job would add a job type and UI for what the mode list already says. Taken apart (the existing `deconstruct` job, worked from beside it) it gives back its mechanism and its worn load. No one keeps an `anyone` trap: 19 makes no rearm or clean job for it, so a spent old cage trap waits until the fortress claims it.
- **Visible once found.** It is drawn as a trap `^`, but red (as a locked door) while it is armed and `anyone`, and only where its tile is discovered, as every building is (23 step 1 draws nothing undiscovered). Its panel says "An old trap of the fortress that stood here: it springs on anyone, your dwarves too. Choose a mode to make it yours, or take it apart." with both modes unmarked and take apart. No announcement on discovery: a dwarf usually discovers it by walking up to it, the red glyph shows it at once, and `TrapTriggered` already announces it when it springs; a discovery announcement would need 19 to watch `TilesDiscovered` for one rare building.
- **Fresh embarks.** Every new path runs only with a ruin record (the `anyone` mode never arises otherwise; the renderer and panel branches only read it), so the golden world hashes (`deepholm`, `emberhall` at 0, 1000, 5000 ticks) and the render goldens are unchanged; the sweep was not run.
- **Found, not fixed: a trap taken apart gives back its load twice** (14's completion writes the loaded units into `settings.inputs`, and 19 also drops the installed load). A ruin's armed trap is placed with only its mechanism as input, so it is not affected; the fix changes fresh embarks that build traps and needs a sweep, so it is a follow-up.
