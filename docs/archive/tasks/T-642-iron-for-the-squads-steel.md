# T-642 — Iron binds the three-year squad's steel once the fuel is there

**Closed:** 2026-11-19, in eb40135e,2cd2b957,ced1544e,21e55deb,e4ee5953,012a9f71,b04d4ec8,b4b64870.

**Source:** [T-619 and T-620's sweep](../../../reports/data/2026-11-19-t619-t620-squad-quarters-and-fuel.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Why

With the coal dug and smelted (T-620), fuel no longer binds the strong squad's steel: 7 of the 52 peril-2 and peril-4 fortress-timelines had under five charcoal in stock on a weekly average, against 31 before. Iron does: 38 of the 52 had under five iron bars on a weekly average (19 before), and in `three-years-p2-1` and `three-years-p4-6` at day 300 the furnaces held 38 to 40 charcoal and 28 to 30 coal with no iron bar. The smelter made 14.4 steel bars a timeline (10.1 before); 4 of 52 had the 50 bars of five kits.

## What to do

Measure where the iron ore goes and how fast the miners bring it (the ore faces opened a season, the drifts' length, the smelter's time between coal, iron and steel), and what a player does with too little: open more iron faces while the steel is owed, mine the deep band's ore sooner, ask the Mountainhome for iron bars ahead of other sundries, or melt iron goods the fortress does not need. Do what raises the steel without costing the sweep.
