# T-606 — The strong squad does not reach Proficient in three years

**Closed:** 2026-11-18, in 20f4297c,f1b073cd.

**Source:** [T-603's report](../../../reports/data/2026-11-18-t603-strong-squad.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Why

T-603 has the three-year script raise the squad T-601's bars ask for: six soldiers at drill from day 112 (24 adults), two marksdwarves at 30 adults, steel kits from the forge and the Mountainhome. No fortress-timeline of the sweep cleared a bar. Steel came for five soldiers in 3 of 52 timelines; skill never did. A soldier at drill gains about a level in one to two weeks: 12 xp a session (`TRAIN_XP`, *unmeasured*), 15 with a Warden, about 1.3 sessions a day once needs, sleep and the odd one out are counted, and 3,700 xp from 0 to Proficient, some 200 days. Starting earlier (16 adults from day 84) cost peril-4 fortresses 25 living dwarves a timeline and still reached no bar.

## What to do

Measure training pace in a fortress, not an arena: sessions a day per soldier at drill and what keeps it away (needs, sleep, social, distance from the barracks to food and beds). Then decide, with the owner, which of these a player has:

- training is meant to take a soldier to Proficient in about a year of drill, and `TRAIN_XP` (or the session length) is set from that measurement, marking the number measured;
- or the pace is right, and the three-year script cannot field the bar's squad: T-601's bars stay a target the sweep never meets, and peril 4's ambushes are hidden from for three years (T-593's 0.9 dwarves an ambush).

## Done

A measured pace in 16's tuning table, the decision written there or in the testing strategy's three-year section, and the sweep's squad lines after it.
