# T-619 — The three-year script's soldiers live far from their barracks

**Closed:** 2026-11-19, in e09118d0.

**Source:** [T-615's measurement](../../../reports/audits/2026-11-18-t615-soldiers-needs.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Why

In `three-years-p4-4` the barracks and the hearth hall are on one level, the food and drink two levels up, and the bedrooms at the far end of the barracks' level, joined by one stair: a soldier's bed is 40 to 70 steps from the barracks. Even its quickest soldiers spend 13 to 17% of the day walking back to drill and stand there under a quarter of the day; a slow one (Agility under 60) spends the night walking to its bed and stands at drill 1 to 4% of the day. In the compact `p2-2` the same soldiers stand there 25 to 40%.

## What to do

Measure, with T-606's probe, whether the script should give the strong squad what a player would: a food and drink stockpile beside the barracks, and the soldiers' beds (a dormitory or bedrooms) off the same corridor. Do it if it raises the squad's pace in the fortresses where the barracks lies far from the beds and the larder, without costing the sweep.

## Done

The probe's barracks share by soldier before and after on `p4-4`, `p4-1` and `p2-2`, and the sweep's verdict.
