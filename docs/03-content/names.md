# Names

**Owner:** content (`names`) · **Depends on:** [`../00-vision/vision.md`](../00-vision/vision.md), [`../02-systems/10-dwarves.md`](../02-systems/10-dwarves.md), [`../02-systems/01-world-generation.md`](../02-systems/01-world-generation.md)

All names are generated by picking from lists with the rng stream of the caller (`embark.party`, `immigration`, `worldgen.names`). No name is used twice among living dwarves; the generator retries up to 20 times then appends a numeral suffix in words ("the Second").

## Region names

`flavor + landform`, where `flavor` depends on the biome and `landform` on elevation and water. A biome without a flavor group, including ocean, produces the empty name `''`.

| Biome group | Flavor words |
|---|---|
| grassland, steppe, savanna | Green, Wide, Long, Wind, Horse, Sun, Dry, Golden, Whistle, Amber |
| forest, dark forest, tropical | Old, Deep, Bramble, Oak, Ash, Shadow, Thorn, Moss, Elder, Twisted |
| hills, mountain, alpine | High, Grey, Stone, Craggy, Iron, Cold, Cloud, Eagle, Bald, Sharp |
| taiga, tundra, glacier | Frost, White, Pine, Bitter, Snow, Silent, Pale, Wolf, Bone, Still |
| wetland | Mire, Reed, Fog, Sallow, Eel, Heron, Black, Sunken, Sedge, Willow |
| desert, badlands | Red, Dust, Bare, Scorch, Salt, Crack, Bleached, Ember, Burnt, Hollow |

| Landform | Condition | Words |
|---|---|---|
| water | river or lake | mere, brook, ford, fen, pool, run |
| high | mountain or alpine | fell, peak, crag, tor, spur |
| hills | hills | down, howe, knoll, ridge, brae |
| low | otherwise | hollow, vale, moor, field, reach |

Examples: Coldmere, Ashfell, Greenhollow, Thornbrook, Dustreach, Whitecrag.

## Dish words

Meal names are "{ingredient} {dish}" with `dish` by meal tier: easy → stew, mash, broth, fry; fine → roast, pie, pottage, hash; lavish → feast, banquet, glory, delight. The ingredient is the first input's retained material name. The dish word is selected by the stable hash of the item id modulo the tier's word count; no extra item field or random draw is needed.

## Squad names (defaults)

The Hearthguard, The Deep Wardens, The Iron Line, The Ember Watch, The Gate Axes, The Cold Bolts, The Stone Shields, The Raven Company. The player renames freely.

## Caravan and hostile names

Traders and hostiles are unnamed ("a Hillfolk merchant", "a gloam captain"). A gloam captain who survives a raid and returns in a later one is named on the second appearance from the hall-name lists with a Gloam prefix (Gnash, Sneer, Rat, Grub, Mire, Skulk) and shown as "Gnashvein the captain, returned"; the legend records them.
