# T-NEW — The three-year fortress has too little steel for the strong squad

**Source:** [T-606's sweep](../../reports/data/2026-11-18-t606-sweep.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Why

With T-606's training pace, five soldiers armed in metal stand at Proficient in 8 of the sweep's 52 fortress-timelines at perils 2 and 4, but no timeline clears a T-601 bar: the bar asks five soldiers in a steel helm, body armour, greaves, weapon and shield, and no fortress had more than four in steel at Proficient. Steel body armour was on 23 of 165 soldiers in metal at year 3; five or more wore it in 2 of the 52. T-603's report found the smelter making 0 to 3 steel bars over year 3 (flux 1 to 5 in stock) and the Mountainhome's caravans bringing little of the requested steel.

## What to do

Measure where the steel goes and what limits it in the sweep's peril-2 and peril-4 fortresses: flux mined and bought, iron bars to spare, the smelter's alloy order's waits, the Mountainhome's caravans (how often, what they bring of the requests, what the fortress can pay). Then decide whether the script plays steel as a player would and fix the script, or whether the economy (20 § Requests, 05's flux) makes a steel kit for five out of reach in three years, and record the decision in the testing strategy's three-year section.

## Done

The sweep's verdict lines for the bars, the steel by fortress, and the decision written down.
