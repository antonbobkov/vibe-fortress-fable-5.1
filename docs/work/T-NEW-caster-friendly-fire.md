# T-NEW — A runecaster behind its squad shoots its own soldiers

**Source:** [the caster arena](../../reports/audits/2026-11-02-t470-caster-arena.md) · **Size:** S · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

The specification is [25 Runecraft](../02-systems/25-runecraft.md) § Casting, which says a bolt is 16's projectile and "a bystander can be an ally: friendly fire is real by construction".

## What was found

In the combat-pace ambush arena (three Novice soldiers in full kit and a Proficient runecaster with an iron staff of embers, against a peril-2 ambush of four), the caster's bolts hit a fortress creature in 14 of 24 fights: 28 hits for 443 damage, against 95 hits for 1,940 on the raiders. A frost staff hit allies 39 times (208 damage) and a wand 26 times (193). The squad still does better with the caster than without (soldiers dead a day later 0.88, 0.63 and 0.63 against 1.08), so the rule is not broken, but a player reading the combat log sees the caster burn its own line one fight in two. Against a raid of ten in the open it is worse: 82 hits on its own side for 1,357 damage against 84 on the raiders for 1,495, in 23 fights of 24.

## What to decide

Whether this is the intended weight of friendly fire, or whether a caster holds a bolt while an ally stands on the line to its target (as 25 already rules for Firebloom in T-473: it is never thrown into a square with a fortress creature). Measure the choice in the same arena before and after.

## Done

- The rule chosen is in 25 § Casting and 16 § Ranged resolution.
- The arena is re-run and the figures replace the caster rows of `balance-status.md`.
