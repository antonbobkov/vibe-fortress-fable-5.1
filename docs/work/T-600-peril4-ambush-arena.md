# T-600 — Can a strong squad meet a peril-4 ambush?

**Source:** [T-593's measurement](../../reports/data/2026-11-18-t593-peril4-ambushes.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Decided

Peril 4's ambushes stay as they are (T-593): each kills as many dwarves as a peril-2 one, and the three-year script hides from every one of them. This task measures the answer the script does not use; it changes no game rule unless the arena shows the squad cannot win.

## Why

The three-year script's squad of five stands down for any ambush larger than its armed soldiers (T-575), and every peril-4 ambush is six or more, so the sweep never sees a squad fight one. The arenas measured three soldiers against an ambush of four. The owner's bar for a strong squad (2026-11-13: about five fighters in steel, a few archers and a wizard, which should have no problem with wildlife) has not been set against six to ten Gloam warriors and archers in iron.

## What to do

An arena in the manner of T-544's wild-squad arena (`reports/audits/2026-11-13-t544-wild-squad-arena.md`): five Proficient soldiers with axe, shield, helm, mail shirt and greaves, three Proficient marksdwarves with crossbows, and a Proficient runecaster with a staff of embers, all in steel, sent against a peril-4 ambush (60% warriors, 40% archers, tier-3 gear) of 6, 8 and 10 in the open, 24 fights each. Report wins (the ambush breaks or is wiped), soldiers dead a day later and blood lost. If the squad wins most fights at every size, record the measurement in [balance-status.md](../balance-status.md) and in 03 `balance.md` § Combat pace. If it loses at 8 or 10, repeat at 80% warriors and 20% archers and report both; the composition change is then a balance change for the owner, measured by a sweep.

## Done

The arena's figures in the balance tables, with its report.
