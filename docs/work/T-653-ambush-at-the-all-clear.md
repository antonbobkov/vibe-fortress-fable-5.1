# T-653 — An ambush springs as the alert comes down

**Source:** [T-650's measurement](../../reports/data/2026-11-20-t650-peril-4-immigration.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Why

In every sweep T-650 read (master before T-639 and T-642, each alone, and both), 4 to 6 ambushes of the 80 three-year fortress-timelines sprang 40 to 100 ticks after the script lowered an alert: hidden by the gate through the alert (a raid's, or another ambush's), they sprang on the first dwarves to come out. They killed 19 to 53 a set of four sweeps, against 229 to 234 for all the other ambushes. In the merged master's sweep (44661428169) one of them, in `three-years-p4-1` at shift 100 on day 295, killed 31 after a raid of 20 had looted and left; with another, in `p4-4` at shift 150 on day 261, which sprang while a wolf alert was up and three soldiers were out on the hunt (13 dead), they carry nearly all of that sweep's rise in deaths (121.75 against 110.25 with T-639 alone).

## What to do

Look at a few of these springs in a probe (where the ambush waited, whether the gate's watch or dogs were at their posts when the alert came down, who came out first) and answer as a player would: for example, the watch and the dogs take their posts before the civilians go out, or after a raid the alert stays up until the watch has looked; and an ambush that springs during a wildlife alert is answered as an ambush (the hunters called in, the gate shut). Keep what lowers the deaths without costing the living in the sweep.
