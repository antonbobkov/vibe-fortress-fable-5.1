# T-468 — Runecraft 1: glowcap, the laboratory and rune vials

**Source:** runecraft [design](../../reports/designs/2026-11-01-runecraft-magic-system.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** T-467 · **Status:** open

## What to do

The first half of the design's phase 1 and § 1. The low-level spec from T-467 governs where it differs from the design.

- The `glow` light value in 07: a dark tile covered by a glowstone lamp. Glowcap crop and wild patches. Seeds from the Mountainhome.
- `rune_vial` item, `rune_cyan` colour, `alchemy` skill and labor.
- Workshop `alchemist` with `distill_rune` and `grind_glowstone`. The other two recipes wait for T-476 unless they cost nothing to add.

## Done

- A glowcap on a dark tile under a lamp grows; with the lamp removed, or on a lit tile, it does not.
- Two glowcap make one vial at the laboratory, end to end through a dwarf.
- A fortress that never cuts a glowstone behaves as before, apart from the wild patches. The annual instrument is run once and any move is explained.
