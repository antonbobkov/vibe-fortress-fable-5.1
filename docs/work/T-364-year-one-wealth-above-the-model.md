# T-364 — Year-1 wealth of the economy fixture is two to three times the model

**Source:** B-008, re-filed by T-145's peril-0 sweep, [the report](../../reports/audits/2026-10-17-t145-peril-0-sweep.md) · **Size:** M · **Needs:** — · **Status:** open

## Why

`balance.md` § Wealth curve says a competent fortress holds 4,000–8,000 marks at the end of year 1. The economy fixture at default priorities holds 8,380–23,187 (median 18,740) on the seven peril-0 seeds, and 14 / 25 / 37 dwarves on the main seed. Years 2 and 3 are near or inside their bands (year 3: five of seven seeds in 35,000–60,000). The overshoot is the first year's.

## What to do

1. Break year-1 wealth down by source (embark, stone furniture and blocks, crafts, meals and drink, metal) on the main seed and two sweep seeds, from the fixture's own measurements.
2. Decide which is wrong: the model's year-1 band (it predates the fixture's export crafts and kitchen), or a value multiplier in `items.md` / `materials.md`. Write the decision into `balance.md` § Wealth curve; a value change re-measures the sweep.

## Done

- Year-1 wealth sits inside the model's band on the sweep, or the band in `balance.md` is restated from the measurement with its reason.
