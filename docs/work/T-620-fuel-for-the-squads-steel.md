## Why

With the script keeping its iron, flux and a charcoal burner for the strong squad's steel, the smelter makes 13 steel bars a fortress-timeline and five kits (50 bars) are reached in 5 of 43 fortresses. Charcoal binds first: 20 of the 43 had under five charcoal in stock on a weekly average and 15 under fifteen logs; a steel bar burns two charcoal, and the forge one a piece.

## What to do

Measure where the wood goes (the furnace, the carpenter's barrels and beds, the caravans) and what a player does with too little: ask the Mountainhome for coal and charcoal (its sundries) in place of the steel kit pieces it seldom brings (0.5 a timeline), mine and smelt coal where the map has it, plant or cut further, or burn the half fuels. Do what raises the steel without costing the sweep.
