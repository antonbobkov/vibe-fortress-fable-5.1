## The three-year balance table (T-M8-06)

This is measured on the final source in this gate run: the `three_years` scenario and the 20-seed
sweep (`test/perf/three_years_seeds.test.ts`). The fortress is the shared scripted fortress in
`test/scenarios/three_years_fortress.ts`. It builds bedrooms ahead of population, a dining hall, a
tier-3 hearth hall, drink stockpiles, a two-door gate with a squad of three and, at perils 2 and 4, an
embark anvil and a forge. The scenario asserts only the deterministic guarantees (mood floor, no
spiral, caps, no peril-0 deaths). Everything else is reported here, and gaps above 30% are
balance findings for the owner (`DECISIONS.md` B-008 … B-012). No balance constant was changed
except by owner decisions F-10, F-12 and F-14.

**What the table says.** The rules now hold together at every peril:
- **Raids.** Raids loiter at a locked gate and leave (F-13, the door-lock fix, F-15).
- **Shelter.** No civilian freezes outside shelter (F-16).
- **Combat.** Combat pursues and resolves (the engage fix).
- **Wealth.** Wealth counts only what the fortress owns (D-219).

The remaining gaps are balance, and they are the owner's to tune:
- **Wealth plateaus near 3,800.** The scripted fortress mines no ore and runs no export crafts, so
  nothing compounds. Renown therefore stays at 4–7, raids stay small and the immigration bonus
  never grows (B-008, B-010).
- **Mood is in the upper Fine band.** Hall spirit sits around 55, so F-14's threshold admits
  immigration on some days but no wave landed in the measured windows (B-009, B-011).
- **Consumption runs about 40% under the model** (B-012; F-10 derived its rates for Endurance 50).
- **Perils 2 and 4 are attritional.** The gate holds, but squad and outdoor losses accumulate
  against a fortress that cannot armour more than one soldier from five embark bars.

`reports/M8-mood-diagnosis.md` has the full investigation, and `reports/M8-wealth-diagnosis.md`
and `reports/M8-engage-diagnosis.md` cover the two bugs it led to.
