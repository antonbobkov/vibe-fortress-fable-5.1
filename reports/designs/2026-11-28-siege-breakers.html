Siege Breakers

Gloam raids and sieges bring specialists that open, break or dig through what the fortress hides behind. They come late at low peril and early at high peril, and each has a warning and something the player can build against it.

Owner's request: "For lock-in raids, let's add mechanic to break that. Opponent gets different units with different skills: lockpicking doors, breaking walls, digging through dirt and (most powerful) digging through rock. This would only come at high perils/later in the game." It fits the decision of 2026-11-28 that peril grows with renown (T-802). Nothing here is built yet. No code, spec or backlog is changed.

1The problem

At perils 3 and 4, locking the doors under the Hearth alert is the only safe answer, and it is safe without fail. In playtest 4 (peril 4), four threats met behind locked doors killed no one. A siege of 27 stood at the gate for five days at "Morale: steady", never fought, and left with 51 goods. In playtest 6 (peril 3), a siege of 30 did the same. Every raid "looted", and the traps never mattered. In playtest 7 (peril 2, Years 4 to 6), the same raid of 16 came each spring and looted a locked-in fortress: "Fair and safe, but dull."

What already exists. Trollkin (`D`, sieges only), trolls, the hollow stalker, the Hollow Maw and the magma wyrm already break locked doors, taking a flat DOOR_BREAK_TICKS 600 whatever the door is made of (14 § Doors and hatches). A trollkin breaks a door only when it happens to stand next to one, though. Trollkin are large, so they cannot use stairs, and nearestReachable parks the siege on the tile before the gate. On master in T-518 they stood one level down "next to no door". When one did reach a door, the siege walked in and 31 to 40 dwarves died. The spec already has a slot for this (18 § Extension points: "Breaching sieges: fill the breach behavior slot"). It also has a model for a role that comes only from a given year: hexers, at peril 3 from Year 3 and peril 4 from Year 2 (25 § Hexers).

2The breaker roles

All four are Gloam, so the threat stays with the one raider civilisation. Each role is an extra member on top of the party's size, as the captain and hexers are. It walks with its party, and at the loiter tile it goes to work on whatever stands between the party and its goal, the hearth (18 § Party AI). Each role is a content row with a first year per peril, so any one of them can be turned off without touching the others.

3The arms race

Each breaker defeats one kind of waiting, and each has an answer the player can build. No answer is "wait longer". Every answer costs something: bars, mechanisms, a mason's time, a squad's attention or a fight.

Lock the doors (today's answer)→beaten by the picklock, which opens a wooden or stone door in half a day to a day
Forge a metal door, build an airlock, chain a dog or set a cage→beaten by the ram, which breaks even metal in two days
Moat and drawbridge, a stair gate, a kill corridor, a drowning chamber→beaten by the sapper, which goes under a moat cut only into soil and around a gate cut into soil
Gate and halls in rock, the moat dug to rock, constructions lining the soil→beaten by the delver, which goes through rock, slowly and loudly
Sound the rock, then meet it: a squad at the tunnel face, traps in the tunnel, a second line inside, built walls around the hearth hall→the squads finally have a fight they can choose and win

Answers weighed

  • Traps in tunnels: yes. A tunnel is a one-wide corridor the enemy made for you. Once it is heard, its line is known, and a weapon or cage trap at its mouth is the cheapest answer.
  • Moats: yes, but a moat stops the sapper only if it is dug to rock. That one rule tells the player why digging deeper matters.
  • Guarded chokepoints and squads meeting a sapper: yes, and this is the point. The breakers give the squads a target that is weak, alone and announced, rather than a raid of 16 in the open.
  • Sounding the rock: yes, and it is automatic in v1. Any dwarf within 12 tiles hears digging, and a dwarf with the mining labor hears it within 24. No new building is needed.
  • Metal-clad or reinforced doors: yes, using the existing materials. A metal door cannot be picked and takes the ram two days. Stone sits between wood and metal. No new building row is needed.
  • Smoothed or engraved walls being harder: not as armour, because room quality and defence would pull the same job two ways. Instead, a delver that reaches an engraved wall stops to deface it. That buys a few hours and costs the engraving. It is a trade-off the owner may enjoy (see § 5).

5Fairness and fun

What keeps it from feeling like a cheat

  • Predictable target. Every breaker works toward the party's existing goal, the hearth. A door breaker takes the door that nearestReachable already searched through, which is the gate the party stands at. A digger plans its route once, the cheapest by dig time from the loiter tile toward the hearth, and keeps it unless blocked. That way the direction in the warning stays true.
  • It takes time. Picking takes half a day to a day. Ramming takes half a day to two days. A sapper digs about 10 soil tiles in a day, and a delver about 8 rock tiles in one to two days. A siege stays five days today (LOITER_DAYS_SIEGE). Breaker work holds that clock while the breaker makes progress, for at most 3 more days, so a metal door or a tunnel the player blocked still sends the party home.
  • Noise and warning. Three warnings come in turn: the warning a day ahead names the roles; the first sound of work starts the alert; "close" or "the lock gives" is a danger line. A heard tunnel is drawn on the map. Nothing breaks in silently.
  • Caps. A raid brings at most 1 picklock and a siege 2. A siege brings 1 ram crew. A party brings at most one kind of digger: up to 2 sappers or 1 delver. At most one delver comes a year. If the breaker is killed, caged, downed or put to flight, its work stops, and in v1 no one else takes it over. Breakers are weak fighters with high nerve thresholds, so they run early.
  • Pathing stays honest. Diggers never dig a tile they could not dig as a dwarf would: no constructions, no deepsilver, no tile next to fluid they have already seen. The terrain changes go through 03's terrain API like the Deep's emergence (18 § The Deep).

Characterful outcomes (candidates for fun-features)

  • The sapper digs into the brook. Water it could not see behind the last wall floods its own tunnel. A delver that strikes the Sea of Fire's rim fills its tunnel with magma.
  • The delver opens a sealed cavern. Playtest 7's cavern 1 held 13 monsters that never left their pocket in six years. A Gloam tunnel lets them out, and they meet the Gloam first.
  • The picklock picks the wrong door. It works the nearest locked door on its way, which may be the tomb's, the animal pen's (the cows walk out among the raiders) or the spider ranch's room.
  • The delver stops to deface an engraving. A gloam delver has defaced an engraving of the Thane wishing for deepfire. It buys hours and costs quality.
  • Trophies. The ram is left behind as a log, and the delver's stolen iron pick is loot, so a poor fortress might get its second pick from the Gloam. A tunnel left behind belongs to the fortress: a sally port, or something to wall up.

6Risks and measures

  • One open door is a massacre. T-518 lost 31 to 40 dwarves to one broken gate door. The breakers are slow and announced so that the player has time. The three-year script must answer each one as a player would: a second line inside, a metal gate door, the squad posted at a heard tunnel. Without that, the sweep measures neglect, not the rule.
  • The balance sweep (suite=sweep, 20 seeds × 4 shifts): living at least 1046.63 and deaths at most 132.5, and each change paired within about 8 deaths of its base. Master is already drifting (T-814: 108.5 → 121.75), so this work should follow T-814. Breakers add danger by design, so the deaths may need a reference moved by decision, as T-580 did. That is an owner call, made with the figures.
  • The defence check (suite=defence_seeds, 8 seeds × 8 cadences): at least 5 of 8 seeds end every announced raid repelled or wiped, and on at least 4 of 8 a soldier kills a hostile. The kill count should rise, which is the purpose of the change.
  • Arenas per role, in the combat-pace style with 24 trials per variant: the ram against wooden, stone and metal doors; the picklock against a dog, a candle and a cage; a sapper against a soil gate and a rock gate; a delver against a heard tunnel with and without a squad.
  • The long game. The sweep plays three years, so delvers below peril 4 fall outside it. T-802's six-year probe at peril 2 covers Years 4 to 6. The wildlife instrument (suite=instrument, peril 0, Year 1) should not move at all, which makes it a no-change check.
  • Cost and saves. A digger's route search runs once per breaker and is bounded. New party and hostile fields are optional, and missing means none, as earlier party fields were.

7Open questions for the owner

  1. Should the ram break constructed walls as well as doors? That would change the hint "Trollkin break doors; walls and bridges hold". Yes, walls too, in a time set by the material. A raised drawbridge and natural rock still hold, and the hint becomes "…; bridges and rock hold".
  2. Can a picklock open a metal door or a hatch? Hatches yes, as doors. Metal doors no, since they are barred: they give the forge a defensive job, and the ram still breaks them, slowly.
  3. Should the diggers ever dig through constructions? No. Built walls and floors stop sappers and delvers, which gives the player one certain answer to digging and leaves walls to the ram.
  4. How should the schedule scale at low peril? As in the pacing table: a first year per peril, a renown floor per role, and T-802's renown-60 step to the next row. Peril 0 gets picklocks from Year 5 and nothing in serene_vale.
  5. Should the delver be a Gloam or a beast the Gloam drive (a rock-gnawing thing)? A Gloam with a stolen pick. That keeps one raider faction, makes the pick loot, and keeps it killable by a squad.
  6. Should breaker work extend a party's stay? Yes, while it is making progress, for at most 3 more days. A blocked or killed breaker sends the party home on today's timer.

8Rough phasing

This work comes after T-802 (peril grows with renown) and T-814 (master's drift), so that each phase is measured against a stable base. Each phase is measured and adopted on its own.

P1 · The purposeful ram. Siege trollkin go to the gate door on purpose and work it, with break time by door and wall material, the warning and panel lines, the hint change, and the three-year script's metal gate and second line. Sweep and defence check. This is the smallest step, and it alone ends "the siege that only waits".
P2 · The picklock. The role row, the pick action and its noise line. Metal doors cannot be picked, and hatches can. Cage, dog and candle counters, with arenas.
P3 · The breach framework and the sapper. The digger route plan, tunnel progress, sounding, the map's dotted tunnel and the "Tunnel:" panel line. Breakthrough sends the party back to approach, which changes 18's rule that only a broken door does. The loiter clock is held while work progresses. The sapper is built on this framework.
P4 · The delver. Rock digging on the same framework, one a year, plus the fun outcomes: fluids, sealed caverns and engravings. The six-year probe at peril 2 and peril 4.
P5 · Pacing and the script. The pacing rows in threat-schedules.md, the schedule preview row, and the three-year script answering each breaker as a player would. A final sweep, defence check and instrument, and entries in docs/fun-features.md.
