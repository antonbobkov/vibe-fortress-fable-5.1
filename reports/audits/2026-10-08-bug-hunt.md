# Audit: bug hunt

**Task:** T-115 · **Date:** 2026-10-08 · **Commit examined:** `afdbe66` · **Kind:** audit; no source, test, fixture, constant or spec file was changed

## Summary

The hunt found 13 confirmed bugs that break a fortress or give a wrong result, and 9 cosmetic ones. Most of them have one root: things that belong to a creature are not handled when the creature dies or leaves the map.

- **One bug breaks a fortress.** A dwarf who dies while holding a pick or an axe takes the tool out of the world. The stocks still show it. No other dwarf can ever fetch it, and every dig or chop job stays blocked with `no_tool`. It happens in the three-year fixture at peril 4 by day 30.
- **Death and departure leave things behind.** A dead soldier's weapon stays owned by the dead dwarf for ever. A buried dwarf's clothes and gear stay in the stock counts and in wealth, and are nowhere. A dead dwarf keeps its bedroom. A meal reserved by a dwarf who dies stays reserved. A raider's gear lies on its arrival tile while it walks, and stays there for ever after it leaves.
- **The squad equipment code writes item records directly.** The stock count of available items goes wrong and never recovers, and a container keeps listing an item that left it.
- **Four commands throw instead of rejecting.** The throw aborts the tick and drops every command queued behind it. Two more commands accept a value that makes the game impossible to save.
- **Save and load, and determinism:** the results are in "Long runs".

A second agent can run every reproduction below. The probes are saved under [`../data/`](../data/) and the section "The probes" says how to run them.

## Confirmed bugs, ranked

Severity is one of: **breaks a fortress**, **wrong result**, **cosmetic**.

### Breaks a fortress

#### 1. A dwarf who dies holding a tool takes the tool out of the world

- **Reproduction (measured):** `death2.probe.ts`. Flat-test map 48 × 48, seed `t115-death2`. One miner with only `mining` on, one iron pick on the ground, a channel designation of eight tiles. The miner takes the pick at tick 25 (`Worker.toolId` set, `Equipment.weapon` 0). The miner is killed at tick 2,430 with a `DamageDealt` event of twice its torso hit points, as `t117-death-consumers.test.ts` does. A new miner is spawned and four more tiles are designated.
- **What happens:** the pick keeps `carriedBy` = the dead dwarf's id. It has no `Position` and is in no container; `items.tileOf` returns −1. The corpse's `wornItems` holds the three clothes and not the pick. The stock reader says `pick: total 1, available 1`. The new miner never gets a tool. The four channel jobs are `blocked: no_tool` three days later and 27 days later, after the corpse has rotted.
- **What the spec says:** 13 § Forbid, dump, melt, claim: "Items dropped by dead dwarves and hostiles are not forbidden by default … items *worn by a corpse* stay with the corpse until the corpse is buried, rots, or is butchered". A held tool is neither dropped nor worn. 11 § Job steps: "Workers keep their tool after the job" and "A held tool remains unavailable to searches through `Worker.toolId` and the item's `carriedBy`".
- **Cause (read):** `src/systems/health/tick.ts:57–65` builds `wornItems` from `Equipment` only. `Worker.toolId` and `Worker.carrying` are not in it, and nothing else releases them on `CreatureDied`.
- **How often:** every death of a dwarf that holds a tool outside the weapon slot. Measured in the balance fixture too: on `three-years-p4-1` at day 30 the fortress axe 16777232 is `carriedBy` a dwarf that no longer exists (`gear.probe.ts`, last lines).
- **Why it ranks first:** a fortress embarks with two picks and two axes. Each lost one is gone until a forge and an anvil exist. This may be part of why the peril-2 and peril-4 fixtures stop digging and felling (T-140 to T-145); that link was not measured.
- **What a hauled item does when its carrier dies was not established:** the probe's hauler had not picked anything up when it was killed.

### Wrong result

#### 2. A dead soldier's claimed gear stays owned by the dead dwarf for ever

- **Reproduction (measured):** `death.probe.ts`, seed `t115-death`. Three dwarves, one pick, two short swords (one in a barrel). `CreateSquad`, `SetSquadMembers` with two of them, `SetSquadMode active`. One soldier claims the pick as its weapon. That soldier is killed at tick 2,411.
- **What happens:** the pick goes to the corpse (`carriedBy` = corpse) and keeps `flags = owned`, `ownerId` = the dead dwarf. On day 28 the corpse has rotted and the pick lies on the ground at (21, 20, 32), still `owned` by the dead dwarf. `pick: total 1, available 0`. A new miner has no tool and six channel jobs are `blocked: no_tool` for 26 days.
- **What the spec says:** 13 § Forbid…: `owned` "belongs to a dwarf". 16 § Equipment: "Claimed items are `owned`". Nothing says ownership outlives the owner; 13 says dropped items of dead dwarves are usable ("not forbidden by default").
- **Cause (read):** `src/systems/combat/equipment.ts:292–294` sets the flag; no `CreatureDied` consumer clears `ownerId` or the flag. `canEquip` (`equipment.ts:117`) and 13's searches skip owned items.
- **How often:** every death of a squad member that had claimed anything.

#### 3. Squad equipping bypasses the item API: stock counts go wrong and containers keep listing the item

- **Reproduction (measured):** `death.probe.ts`, the lines "after squad equip, nobody dead" and "end". No one has died yet at the first check.
- **What happens:** after two soldiers claim the pick and a sword, `items.available('pick')` is 1 and a recount gives 0; `items.available('short_sword')` is 2 and a recount gives 1. At the end the second sword has been claimed out of the barrel: the barrel's `contents` still lists it, the item's `containerId` is 0, and `available` is 2 against a recount of 0. The same drift shows in the fuzz world (`axe: available 2, recount 0`; the wagon lists an axe that is not in it).
- **What the spec says:** 13 § Stock counts: `available` is "not forbidden, not reserved, not owned", "maintained incrementally". `testing-strategy.md` § Property tests: "`counts` equals a recount from entities".
- **Cause (read):** `claimItemForSoldier` and `dropItemAtBarracks` (`src/systems/combat/equipment.ts:247–300`) write `flags`, `ownerId`, `carriedBy`, `containerId` and remove `Position` on the record itself. They do not call `setOwner`, `takeOut` or `attachToCarrier`. `refreshDerivedIndexes` "changes no record, count or event" (13 § Interfaces).
- **Effect:** the stocks panel and every `maintain` order that reads `available` count weapons and armour that soldiers hold. A recount (a load does not recount: the counts are saved) never happens.
- **How often:** every claim.

#### 4. A raider's gear lies on its arrival tile while it walks, and stays there for ever when it leaves the map

- **Reproduction (measured):** `gear.probe.ts`: the three-year fixture, seed `three-years-p4-1`, peril 4, 30 days. A thief arrives at tick 25,201 on tile (0, 0, 32) with an axe, a spear and leggings.
- **What happens:** at tick 25,405 the thief is at (11, 4, 32). Its three items have `carriedBy` = the thief and a `Position` of (0, 0, 32). The item reader and the spatial index list them on that tile. At tick 25,431 the thief leaves the map. The three items remain on (0, 0, 32) on day 30, flagged `foreign | owned`, `ownerId` and `carriedBy` naming an entity that no longer exists. No job can take them: they are owned.
- **What the spec says:** 13 § Concepts: an item has "a `Position` when it lies on the ground … items carried have a `carriedBy`". 18 § Party AI, Retreat: members "despawn there". 16: "Hostile equipment stays on the corpse", which is the death path and works.
- **Cause (read):** `src/systems/threats/gear.ts:147–155` spawns each item on the tile and then writes `carriedBy` on the record without `attachToCarrier`, so the `Position` stays. `despawnMember` (`src/systems/threats/ai.ts:382–420`) destroys the creature and not what it carries.
- **How often:** every hostile that arrives with gear and leaves alive. The long runs count them (see "Long runs").

#### 5. A buried dwarf's worn items stay in the stock counts and in wealth, and are nowhere

- **Reproduction (measured):** `death3.probe.ts`, first test. Flat-test, two dwarves, a coffin in a tomb zone. One dwarf is killed and is buried at tick 118.
- **What happens:** its tunic, trousers and boots have `carriedBy` = the corpse, and the corpse is destroyed by the burial. They have no position 20 days later. `tunic total 2` before and after; wealth 142 before and after.
- **What the spec says:** 13 § Forbid…: worn items "stay with the corpse until the corpse is buried, rots, or is butchered". The rot path drops them on the tile (`src/systems/items/rot.ts:123`). The spec does not say whether burial drops them or buries them; either way they should leave the counts or return to the map.
- **Cause (read):** `src/systems/buildings/burial.ts:117` calls `items.destroy(corpseId, 'buried')`. `destroy` (`src/systems/items/api.ts:649`) empties a container's contents and ignores `Corpse.wornItems`.
- **How often:** every burial. For a soldier it is the weapon and armour too.

#### 6. A dead dwarf keeps its bedroom for ever

- **Reproduction (measured):** `death2.probe.ts`. A bed in a two-tile bedroom zone, `AssignRoom` to one dwarf, the dwarf is killed on day 2. Three living dwarves have no bedroom.
- **What happens:** on day 29 `Room.ownerId` is still the dead dwarf. The daily auto-assignment gives the room to nobody.
- **What the spec says:** 14 § Ownership: "bedrooms with a built bed and no owner, in zone-id order, go to living fortress creatures".
- **Cause (read):** `src/systems/buildings/rooms.ts:444` offers only rooms with `ownerId === 0`; 14 does not subscribe to `CreatureDied` and nothing clears the owner.
- **How often:** every death of a dwarf with a bedroom. After a bad raid most bedrooms are dead.

#### 7. A meal or drink reserved by a dwarf who dies stays reserved for ever

- **Reproduction (measured):** `death.probe.ts` and `death2.probe.ts`. In both, a killed dwarf had reserved one unit of drink (`reservedBy` = the dwarf's id, the convention 10 uses for an eater).
- **What happens:** 26 days later the unit still has the `reserved` flag and `reservedBy` naming the dead dwarf. It is not available and nobody drinks it.
- **What the spec says:** 13: `reserved` is "held by a job; 11 sets and clears".
- **Cause:** not established beyond this: `board.workerDied` releases the reservations of the worker's job, and this reservation is held by the dwarf, not by a job.
- **How often:** once per death in the middle of a meal or drink errand. Two of the five probe deaths.

#### 8. Four commands throw instead of rejecting, and the throw aborts the tick

- **Reproduction (measured):** `commands.probe.ts`, on a fresh flat-test fortress:
  - `SetHuntable { species: 'no_such_species', on: true }` throws `RangeError: creatures: unknown id`;
  - `AddWorkOrder { workshopId: 0, order: null }` throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'recipeId')`;
  - `EditWorkOrder { workshopId, index: 0, patch: null }` throws the same;
  - `DesignateStairwell { x: 1.5, y: 30, zTop: 32, zBottom: 30 }` throws `RangeError: tile index out of range: 297793.5`.
- **What happens when the command is queued:** `world.step()` throws. The tick does not advance (401 stays 401). A `RenameFortress` submitted behind the bad command is never applied: the queue was drained. The next `step()` runs.
- **What the spec says:** `command-catalog.md`: every command has a validation and a rejection reason from the fixed list. T-115: "Every one must be rejected with a reason or applied cleanly, and never throw".
- **Cause (read):** `src/systems/workshops/orders.ts:30` and `:41` read `order.recipeId` and `patch.recipeId` without a null check; the species lookup uses `Table.index`, which throws; the stairwell tile index is computed from a fraction.
- **How often:** only with a malformed command. The UI does not build these. A replay or an import of a damaged command log would.

#### 9. `SetHospitalSupplies` and `SetZoneSetting` accept any value, and a `NaN` makes the game impossible to save

- **Reproduction (measured):** `commands.probe.ts`. `SetHospitalSupplies { zoneId: <hospital>, wants: { cloth: NaN } }` returns `ok`; the next save throws `non-finite number in state: NaN`. `SetZoneSetting { zoneId: <barracks>, key: 'squad', value: NaN }` does the same. `SetZoneSetting { key: 'squad', value: 123456789 }` is accepted with no such squad.
- **Also accepted by `SetHospitalSupplies` (measured, fuzz):** `wants` as `null`, a string, a number, an array, negative counts, unknown keys.
- **What the spec says:** `command-catalog.md`: `SetZoneSetting` "setting key valid for the type"; `SetHospitalSupplies` "hospital zone". The values have no rule in the catalog, so the code stores them raw (`src/systems/health/index.ts:117–131`, `src/systems/buildings/zones.ts:254–265`). A command that leaves a state the game cannot save is not "applied cleanly".
- **How often:** only with a malformed command.

#### 10. A thief that escapes with an item ends its raid as `withdrew`

- **Reproduction (measured):** `gear.probe.ts`, tick 25,431: `ThiefEscaped { id: 16777521, itemId: 16777408 }` and `RaidEnded { outcome: 'withdrew' }` in the same tick.
- **What the spec says:** 18 § Party AI, Retreat: "`looted` (retreat with loot), … `withdrew` (loiter timeout without loot)". The `RaidEnded` announcement is "an alert when the raid escaped with loot, a note for every other outcome".
- **Cause:** not established. Read: `src/systems/threats/ai.ts:841` and `index.ts:459` set `outcome = 'withdrew'` whenever a stealthed thief is spotted, whether or not it already holds loot; `despawnMember` removes the item from `P.loot` before it computes an outcome.
- **How often:** once in the one thief party observed. T-114 counted 13 `withdrew` on this seed in three years.

#### 11. Ambush parties are never hidden, and `AmbushSprung` cannot be emitted

- **Read:** `src/systems/threats/parties.ts:210`: a member is stealthed when its species has the `stealth` flag or its role is `thief`. An ambush party is `gloam_warrior` and `gloam_archer` (`partyCompositions.json`); neither has the flag. `AmbushSprung` is emitted only when a *stealthed* member of an ambush party is spotted (`ai.ts:842`, `index.ts:460`).
- **Measured by T-114:** seven ambush parties in three years at peril 4 and no `AmbushSprung`, in that run or in any test.
- **What the spec says:** 18 § Stealth: "Spotting emits `ThiefSpotted` (alert) or `AmbushSprung` and removes stealth". 18 § Registrations: `AmbushSprung` is a danger announcement. 18 § Arrival: "Thieves, bandits and ambushes give no warning". The spec's stealth list names only `gloam_snatcher` and `blind_stalker`, so the spec itself does not say what hides an ambush. The fix task decides.
- **Effect:** an ambush is a small raid that is announced at the edge. This closes item 3 of T-114's "Things that look like bugs".

#### 12. `SetSquadEquipment` stores an unknown weapon class and armour kind

- **Reproduction (measured):** `commands.probe.ts`: `SetSquadEquipment { squadId, equipment: { weaponClass: 'nope', armor: 'nope', ranged: 'x' } }` returns `ok` and the squad's saved equipment is exactly that. `{}`, `[]` and `{ weaponClass: 5 }` are accepted too (fuzz).
- **What the spec says:** 16 § Squads: `equipment: { weaponClass: preferred | any, armor: metal | leather | any, ranged: boolean }`.
- **Effect:** what a squad with such a preference equips was not established.

#### 13. `LinkStockpile` accepts a source that is not a stockpile

- **Reproduction (measured):** `commands.probe.ts`: `LinkStockpile { zoneId: <stockpile>, sourceZoneId: <the wagon building>, on: true }` returns `ok`; `linkedSources` is `[16777216]`. The fuzz run linked 19 different buildings and zones of other types.
- **What the spec says:** 13 § Stockpiles: links are between stockpiles.
- **Effect:** not established.

### Cosmetic

Grouped into one task.

- **C1. `SetPopulationCap` clamps and returns `ok` where the catalog validates "20–200".** Measured: `n = -5`, `0` and `19` give a cap of 20; `201` and `1e9` give 200.
- **C2. `SetStandingOrder` accepts `toString`, `constructor`, `hasOwnProperty` and `__proto__` as names.** Measured: the first three are then saved with the standing orders. Read: `name in board.standingOrders` (`src/systems/jobs/commands.ts:80`).
- **C3. `AssignPasture` answers `not_tame` for a tame animal that is not a grazer.** Measured for two dogs and a cat. This is the cause of the three `not_tame` rejections in every three-year run that T-114 could not explain: the fixture sends the command for every tame animal.
- **C4. `SetSquadLeader` accepts a dwarf who is not a member.** Measured: leader set, members `[]`.
- **C5. `SetPlateTrigger` accepts a water level of 1.5**, where the catalog says "level 1 to 7". `SetStockpileContainers` accepts `barrel: 1000000000`.
- **C6. A party in state `gone` is never destroyed.** Measured in the long runs: one `Party` entity stays for each party that ever came.
- **C7. A pet keeps `petOf` naming its dead owner.** Measured in the fuzz world (a cat). Whether that cat can adopt again was not established.
- **C8. `command-catalog.md` has a damaged character sequence** in "values outside 1…9 rejected" (the en dash was double-encoded).
- **C9. Rejection reasons for malformed input are uneven.** A non-integer `n` for `SetPopulationCap` is `bad_setting`; other range faults are `out_of_range`. No unknown reason string was returned by any command in 68,000 trials.

### What the checker checks

- **Jobs:** no job with a dead, absent or unconscious worker; worker and job point at each other; no active job without a worker; no target that no longer exists; every reserved item exists and names the job; no job unchanged in an active state for three days.
- **Items:** in at most one place (ground, container, carrier); not nowhere; a container and its contents agree; no item in two containers; no carrier or owner that no longer exists; the `reserved` flag agrees with `reservedBy`, and `reservedBy` names a living job or a living creature; nothing inside a wall; counts positive.
- **Stock and wealth:** the incremental counts and item wealth equal `ItemCore.recount()`.
- **Creatures:** needs within 0 … 100, mood within its bounds, blood and hit points within theirs; nobody inside a wall or on a tile its movement class cannot stand on; no dwarf unchanged in place, behaviour, activity and job for two days while awake; no retreat without a hearth alert; no flight for a day with no hostile on the map.
- **References:** squad members, leaders, kill orders, room owners, workshop jobs and assigned workers, building owners, items and links, pastures, pet owners, crops' plots.
- **Parties:** not loitering past `LOITER_DAYS_RAID` or `LOITER_DAYS_SIEGE`; not active with no living member; not retreating for two days; not on the map for ten days.

"No alert held with no hostile present" is not an engine rule: 16 § Alerts says "There is no automatic alert change". The checker counts the ticks the fixture holds an alert with no hostile on the map and reports the number.

"No command accepted that leaves the state it should have rejected" is way 4.

## Command fuzzing in numbers

Measured, `fuzz.probe.ts`, 250 random trials per command after the field sweeps. The world is a flat-test fortress at tick 50,400 with a caravan at the depot and a trade session open.

- 78 commands, about 68,000 trials.
- Throws: `SetHuntable` (143 trials), `AddWorkOrder` (15), `EditWorkOrder` (4), `DesignateStairwell` (6). No other command threw, in the command or in the two ticks after it.
- Save and load after accepted commands: one failure, `SetHospitalSupplies` (bug 9). `SetZoneSetting` fails the same way when given `NaN` (`commands.probe.ts`).
- Every rejection reason returned is in the fixed list of `command-catalog.md`.
- The checker found nothing new after any accepted command, apart from C4 and the craft job under "Suspected".
- **Commands the fuzz never got accepted**, so only their rejection paths were exercised: `DesignateHarvest`, `AssignPasture`, `TameCaged`, `ReleaseCaged`, `UnlinkMechanism`, `SetHatchMode`, `ReleaseCage`, `AssignCoffin`, `SetTrapMode`. Their world had no ripe crop, no grazer, no caged creature, no hatch and no linked mechanism. `CreateZone`, `LinkMechanism`, `TradeOffer` and `TradeCancel` were accepted once or twice each.
