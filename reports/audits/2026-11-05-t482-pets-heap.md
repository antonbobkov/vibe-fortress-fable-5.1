## The answer

- **The stale walk is fixed.** A walk that has arrived no longer counts as a walk to the animal's own tile, so T-463's spreading rule moves it; the tame brain stops such a walk once the animal is home. T-463's test "dogs whose last walk ended where they stand still spread out" (now with pigs) fails on master and passes.
- **The pile is kept, as a heap.** Up to four idle pets (no grazers, no larger than a wolf: dogs, cats, foxes, chickens) heap on one *favourite spot*: the walkable tile beside the hall's hearth with the most wall, hearth and furniture around it, not a door and not beside one. Curled there they count for no crowd, so the heap slows no one stepping over it; the rest spread by T-463's rule. On `three-years-p0-2` four dogs lie on the hearth's north-west corner from day 20 to day 140 and later, and no hall tile is crowded on any 10-day look.
- **The hall's crowd is mostly gone.** Over the twenty seeds the hall's slowed samples fall from 4.1% to 1.3%, the capped ones from 111,514 to 23,546, and the longest crowded stretch of a hall tile from 379,180 ticks (316 days, every seed) to 23,220 ticks (19 days); 5 of 20 seeds still have a hall tile crowded for more than a day (19 of 19 before). The probes of the two longest name dwarves on those tiles, not animals (below).
- **The annual instrument passes in both samples:** 12.5/9/10/149/7/0/0 and, one tick later, 12.5/3/9/139/7/0/0, against the reference 13/7/10/149/7/0/0. The median falls by half a dwarf in both; in the second the sum falls by 10, within its bound of 12. No guarded figure exceeds its floor; at most two of the five worsen together. Taiga seed 1 lives in both (13 and 13).
- **The twenty-seed file keeps the workflow's set rule, but not the task's.** 20/20 alive at year 1, 7/7 peril-0 grown; **1/7** peril-0 seeds with need deaths: `p0-3` starves 23 dwarves between days 316 and 336 after growing to 67. The task asked for 0/7. The same code one tick later does not starve on that seed (53 living at day 340, no death). Two peril-4 seeds, `p4-2` and `p4-3`, are wiped out by their year-1 sieges (days 174 and 186); one tick later both live (46 and 19 at day 200). Read with the T-308 rule, these are the timeline's chance, which the change moves on every seed from day 20 (the hall's animals stand elsewhere); see [The sweep](#the-sweep).
