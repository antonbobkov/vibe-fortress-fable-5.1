# Audit: a player's years two and three

**Task:** T-635 · **Date:** 2026-11-19 · **Commit examined:** master `c093b876`, and the T-635 branch for the fixes · **Kind:** a UI and UX pass of the mid-game through the real UI, with the small fixes · **Source:** [the T-635 work file](../../docs/work/T-635-midgame-ux.md), after [T-605's first hour](2026-11-18-t605-first-hour-ux.md) and [T-618's first year](2026-11-18-t618-first-year-ux.md)

## The answer

- **A trade can silently never happen.** The Broker was also a soldier of a squad set to Active; `[S-t] trade now` said "The broker is on the way to the depot.", the trade job sat open for five days and the Hillfolk left with nothing bought or sold and no word why (finding 1, filed).
- **The raid is hard to read while it happens.** The first key after a danger line is swallowed (2); combat fills the Alerts tab (7, 8); the Threats panel does not show the coming raid, a kill order leaves no trace, and the end of a raid says neither what was lost nor that the alert can come down (9–12). Filed.
- **Six things were simply wrong and are fixed:** a thief's arrival announced as "!! A raid has arrived!" with the danger pause; content ids in combat and job lines ("Hillfolk_bandit hits Halvar", "hunt wild_horse"); "seen at the the northern edge edge"; a kill order clicked on a raider that picked the fortress's own dog standing on its tile; the reputation log's raw codes ("+5 yearly_drift"); "1 of them children"; and in the legend, "temperate_forest" and "slew 1 enemies".
- **At 30 dwarves and more the panels stop explaining**: Mood shows six letters of each thought and two "Ragnhild Bri"; Animals is 94 single rows; Jobs and trade requests cut and repeat (13–19, filed).
- **The Making Do recipes name one missing input and stop** ("Soap · no tallow"); where tallow and beeswax come from is nowhere (21, filed).
- **Performance holds** at 29 and 80 dwarves: 60 frames a second and every speed delivered, except the 80-dwarf fortress in a fight at 4×, where the simulation fell to 20 ticks a second and the frame rate to 34, and every key that opens a panel at 80 dwarves takes 130–160 ms to show it, twice the 29-dwarf figure (finding 32).

## How it was examined

A Vite dev server in test mode and a headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 (80 × 50 cells), held open by a small Playwright script and driven through the real keyboard and mouse as the e2e tests drive them; the screen's text came from the test hook (`window.__deepholm.text()`), the pictures from Playwright, events from `host.onEvents`. Time passed with the hook's `step`, a quarter day at a time. Saves went in through the Load screen's `[i] Import`.

1. **Hearthhold**, the three-year economy fixture on `three-years-p2-1` (peril 2), saved headless on day 116 (Spring 4, Year 2, 13 dwarves) by a scratch probe that plays `three_years_fortress.ts`, and played from there to Autumn 1 of Year 2: a raid of ten with the warning a day ahead, the Hearth alert and an active squad, a kill order, the end of the raid, a Thane appointed through Units and the whim of summer (deepfire), a wave of eight immigrants, the Mountainhome caravan (spring, lost to the raid) and the Hillfolk caravan (summer, finding 1), two dwarves cut through the hook (`DamageDealt`, two serious cuts each) and followed for nine days, the Making Do recipes in Add order, the Threats, Civilizations, Population, Units, Mood, Jobs and Orders panels.
2. **Vein Delve**, the same fixture on `three-years-p4-1` (peril 4), saved on day 150 (Summer 10, Year 2, 33 dwarves): Mood and Population at 33, a horde of 18 Gloam warned a day ahead, with the Hearth alert (three dwarves killed) and, from a quick save of the evening before, without it (the fortress fell, finding 31); quick save and load at 32 dwarves; the legend.
3. **Deepholm**, the reference fortress of `performance-budget.md` (`test/fixtures/reference-fortress.deepholm`: 80 dwarves, year 3, 25,000 items, two squads, a gate and traps), for the panels at scale, the frame rate and input lag, and saving and loading a big fortress. It is a scripted load, not a played fortress: its scenery (a field of beds) and its numbers (every reputation +0) are not read as findings.

A siege was not reached: the peril-4 seed's siege comes between days 178 and 190, after the raid that ended the fortress in the second timeline, and the Deep was not stirred in any of them (pressure 0). The caverns were seen only through the Threats panel (one breached, 13 monsters).

Severity: **blocks** (the player cannot get the thing done without knowledge the game does not give), **confuses** (the player is misled or stuck for a while), **polish**. The screenshots (`NN-name.png`) were kept outside the repository.

## What changed on the branch

Findings 3–6, 20, 22 and 23 are fixed, with their tests in `test/unit/ui/t635-midgame.test.ts`:

- **A creature in a line** is named by its own name (a blow by the first word) or by its species with an article, never its content id; one gone before its line is "a creature" (16 § Combat log). The Jobs panel names a hunted animal by its species and capitalises a job type with no string of its own ("Plant", "Hunt wild horse").
- **The raid warning** says its edge once.
- **A raid's arrival** has a line per kind, naming its edge ("A Gloam raid has come from the north!", "A Gloam siege has come from the east!", "Hillfolk bandits have come from the west!"); a thief's arrival is not announced and does not pause the game (18 § Player-facing behavior). `RaidArrived` lists its `edge` in the event catalog.
- **A kill order** on a tile with several creatures takes the most hostile: a raider before a wild animal, a fortress creature only when alone (`ui-screens/panels.md` § Military).
- **The reputation log** lists newest first as many entries as fit, each reason in words and dated as the log dates its lines ("-50 caravan member killed (Sp 3)").
- **A wave with one child** says "1 of them a child".
- **The legend** names the biome ("forest") and writes "slew 1 enemy."
