## The answer

- **p4-2 already died in year 1 before the health tasks T-405 to T-415.** It was dead at `7b681df` (the master before T-405), and at T-405, T-409 and T-411. T-413 tipped it alive (3 at day 112), T-414 kept it (2), T-416 did not move it (same world hash), and T-415 tipped it back to 0. The seed is marginal: the same fire and the same raids decide it at every commit, and which dwarf a roll takes decides whether two or three are left.
- **What kills the fortress is the same at every commit: a fire burns every medic.** Between days 36.8 and 38.8, a fire gives 58 to 64 light burns to 9 of the 11 dwarves, among them all five that have the medicine labor. A burn that is not dressed makes a dwarf a patient (17 § Patients and hospitals), and a patient rests and works no job, so nobody dresses or cleans a burn for five days. The first medic is discharged on day 43.3 (43.8 at `ab4d40e`). Each dwarf carries 5 to 11 light burns, and each burn that is not cleaned, sutured or bandaged rolls `INFECTION_PERMILLE` a day once it is a day old, so 5 to 8 of the 9 are infected by day 60. Four to six of them die of the infection (`INFECTION_DEATH_PERMILLE` a day, and nothing cures it without a medic). The raid of 11 or 12 on day 67, with bandits on day 68, then kills most of the rest; in the runs that die out, two or three more starve.
- **None of T-411 to T-415 changes that chain.** A light burn does not bleed, so it was never bandaged (T-405, T-411), and it is exposed, so it asks for cleaning (T-413). No medic is free to end a job (T-414) or to diagnose (T-415). The commits move only the timeline around it: where the fire catches whom (58 burns or 64), and which rolls fall.
