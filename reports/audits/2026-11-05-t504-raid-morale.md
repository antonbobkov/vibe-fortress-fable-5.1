# Measurement: raid morale, raider nerve, and what a squad can do against a raid

**Task:** T-504 · **Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Commits examined:** master `ff0ce34e` (T-466 and T-504's decision) for the before rows; the T-504 branch (`t504-raid-morale`) at `5a73dde1` for the arena and the `defence_year` run with a sally, `f814730c` (the same rules) for the annual instrument and the twenty-seed file, and `f3785cfa` (the same rules; the sally withdrawn, the panel's morale on a line of its own) for `defence_year` and `pp_defence` · **Kind:** a rule change by the owner's decision, with its arena before and after; the morale constants are new and stay *unmeasured* · **Source:** [the T-504 work file](../../docs/archive/tasks/T-504-armed-squad-repels-a-raid.md), [defence repels a raid](2026-11-05-t466-defence-repels-a-raid.md), [careless shooters](2026-11-04-t483-careless-shooter-arena.md)

## The answer

- **Morale.** A raid, a siege, an ambush and a band of bandits carry a morale from 0 to 100 (a siege 100, a raid 90, an ambush 75, bandits 60). Each member killed, downed, caged or fled lowers it by its share of the party (10, 8, 10 and 5 for a party of ten, its worst counting once); losing the last captain costs 30 at once; a trap that lands costs 3 and a hit from one of its own careless archers 2. Each dwarf it fells gives it 5 and each item of loot 2. It breaks at 20, at half the party lost whatever its morale (the old rule, now the floor of the scale), and, leaderless and out of a fight, below 50.
- **Nerve.** Each raider runs on its own once morale falls below its nerve: its species' flee-at-blood percent less 20, plus 0 to 19 from its entity id (no draw). A warrior runs below 20 to 39, an archer below 30 to 49; a captain never runs alone. It drops what it carries, and its flight costs morale again, so one coward can start a rout. The threats panel says `Morale: steady / shaken / wavering / breaking`; a flight gets "A gloam warrior has lost its nerve and runs."
- **A well-armed squad repels a raid now, and pays less for it.** Six Skilled soldiers in iron break a peril-2 raid of ten in the open before it loots in **24 of 24** fights (24 before too) and lose **1.92** soldiers a fight (2.96 before); six Proficient in steel lose **0.58** (1.25). The raid breaks at about 4 losses of 11 instead of 6, and in **23 of 24** fights the rout cascades: about four raiders run on their own before the party breaks.
- **Raids kept their teeth, and got some back.** Three Novices in the open: squad down 24 of 24 both ways, and the raid now loots in **22** (2 before: T-466's captain rule sent a winning raid home without its loot). Six Novices: down in 17 and looted in **11** (0 before; the raid used to break on its losses after winning). A peril-4 raid against six Skilled soldiers loots in **19 of 24** (4 before). The difference is the morale a raid regains as it fells the squad.
- **`defence_year` keeps its set rule:** the announced raid ends `repelled` on **8 of 8** seeds (7 of 8 in T-466's report; the set rule is 5). On every seed the cage trap takes the captain at the gate and the leaderless raid goes home before it loots; no raider dies.
- **Nothing without a party moves.** `pp_defence` on its four seeds gives T-493's record field for field. The annual instrument gives its reference row, 13/7/10/149/7/0/0 (peril 0 in year 1 sends no party). The twenty-seed file keeps its set rule: 20/20 alive at year 1, 7/7 peril-0 grown, 0/7 with need deaths; on the reference's nineteen seeds **120 deaths and 940 living** against 121 and 902, peril 0 unchanged to the dwarf. `p4-3`, which stopped on T-501's siege in the reference, now finishes (5 deaths, 40 living): its siege of 26 loots and leaves on day 186.

## Fun

- **The rout.** Fell the captain and two or three more, and the first coward runs; its flight puts the next one under, and in all but one Skilled or Proficient fight in 24 about four raiders scatter for the edge before the party breaks. In a unit test, setting morale one point under the shakiest raider's nerve sent it and three more running in the same tick.
- **A coward drops its loot.** A raider that loses its nerve runs light: whatever it was carrying lies where it turned.
- **A winning raid takes heart.** Each dwarf it fells steadies it: a raid whose captain went down early but that beat the squad loots again, where T-466's rule sent it home empty-handed.
- **Careless archers rattle their own side.** An arrow in a comrade's back costs morale; at 1.4 to 2.2 such hits a fight it is a small push, but it can be the push.
- **Bandits run first.** A band starts at 60 and its bandits' nerve is 40 to 59: one of three falling sends the others running. In `defence_year` two of the nine bands are now `repelled`; in T-466's rows every band looted.
- **The cage still does it alone.** A cage trap that takes a raid's captain at the gate drops it from 90 to 48 and the leaderless raid goes home before it loots.

## What this does not measure

- A choke point, and sieges with cage traps: no arena or script for them here.
- Whether the morale constants are right: they are the owner's shape with first numbers, *unmeasured*.
- Bandits and ambushes under morale: the arena has neither; the twenty-seed file plays both.
