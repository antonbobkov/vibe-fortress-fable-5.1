# Measurement: beds that rest differently, and the floor at 50

**Task:** T-551 · **Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Kind:** measurement of a rule change (the floor rests at `FLOOR_REST_PERCENT` 50, beds at their `rest`) · **Source:** [the T-369 sleep audit](2026-10-21-sleep-hours.md), [Making Do](../designs/2026-11-06-making-do-content-and-scarcity.md) § 7 Beds and § 11 ("about 3% more working time with dressed beds")

## What was run

`test/perf/sleep_audit.test.ts` (the T-369 probe with the working time added), through `measure.yml -f suite=sleep_audit`: the first 112 days of the three-year economy fixture at peril 0, seeds `three-years`, `three-years-p0-2`, `three-years-p0-3`, played as `three_years_seeds` plays them. It journals every sleep errand (rest at its start, on lying down and at its end, ticks walking and in bed, the floor or the bed and the percent slept at) and the working time: the share of dwarf-ticks with a job in hand. The suite runs it twice: as the fixture plays, and with dressed beds (every day, a linen bedding and pillow put in stock for each wooden bed that still wants them, so the haulers make the beds up).

Every sleep rests at 100 before: the floor and every bed alike.

## After, as the fixture plays (run 43634603322)

| Seed | Working time | Errands a dwarf-day | Hours a day walking / in bed | Floor sleeps | In bed per sleep, median ticks (floor / bed) |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| `three-years` | 33.50% | 1.14 | 4.56 / 2.46 | 222 of 1,356 | 359 / 84 |
| `three-years-p0-2` | 34.59% | 1.16 | 4.64 / 2.27 | 170 of 1,364 | 366 / 78 |
| `three-years-p0-3` | 38.62% | 1.11 | 3.94 / 3.03 | 386 of 1,554 | 341 / 86 |

- **The floor sleeps twice as long, as the rule says.** A floor sleep lies a median 341 to 366 ticks against 136 to 140 before; a bed's is unchanged (78 to 86). The sleep errand takes 6.9 to 7.0 hours a day against 6.2 to 6.5.
- **The working time barely moves.** Pooled over the three seeds, 35.57% against 35.81% (−0.2 points): `three-years` and `p0-3` lose 1.6 and 1.2 points, `p0-2` gains 2.1 as its timeline moves. The fixture's founders sleep on the floor until their bedrooms have beds (12% to 25% of the errands); after that the floor is rare.
- **The opening is where it shows.** In the first 30 days of `three-years` with dressed beds, 157 of 224 sleeps are on the floor and the sleep errand takes 8.4 hours a day. At default priorities (`t143-default-opening`) the carpenter on `three-years` is built 22 days after it is placed, against under 10 before: the two carpentry dwarves are asleep longer while the founders have no beds. A player lays pallets (T-551's hint says so); the fixture does not, and is left as it was.
- `SLEEP_HOURS` is still never the limit: the longest floor sleep is 653 ticks of a cap of 700 (7 × 100 / 50 hours).

## After, with dressed beds (run 43635787966)

Every day a linen bedding and pillow is put in stock for each wooden bed that still wants them, and the haulers make the beds up.

| Seed | Working time | Hours a day on the sleep errand (walking / in bed) | Sleeps at 125 / 100 / floor | In bed per sleep, median ticks (made up / bed / floor) |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `three-years` | 34.06% (+0.56) | 6.73 (4.14 / 2.60) | 447 / 578 / 251 | 78 / 73 / 362 |
| `three-years-p0-2` | 35.13% (+0.54) | 6.90 (4.67 / 2.23) | 109 / 1,080 / 165 | 48 / 82 / 354 |
| `three-years-p0-3` | 38.62% (±0) | 6.72 (4.15 / 2.58) | 459 / 739 / 328 | 59 / 67 / 343 |

- **Dressed beds give about 1% more working time, not 3%.** Pooled, 35.94% against 35.57% with plain beds: +0.37 points, a relative 1.0%. The design's 3% assumed the time in bed is the sleep; here most of the sleep errand is the walk to bed (4 to 4.7 hours of the 6.7 to 6.9), which a softer bed does not shorten, and the made-up beds are a third of the sleeps at most (on `p0-2` a twelfth: its beds are made up late).
- A made-up bed's sleep is a median 48 to 78 ticks against 67 to 82 on a plain bed, the fifth less the rule gives.
