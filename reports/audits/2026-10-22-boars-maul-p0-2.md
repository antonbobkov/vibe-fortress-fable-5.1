## The answer

- **No fault in 08, 10 or 16 decides p0-2's year 3. Where three boars settle decides it.**
  - Two boars arrive on day 225 and make their home chunk beside the gate. A third arrives on day 260.
  - They sleep, idle and wander there for weeks: a median 18 to 20 tiles from the gate, as close as 2.
  - A territorial boar goes for any dwarf within `TERRITORY_RADIUS` (5). It is faster than a dwarf (0.18 against 0.15 tiles a tick) and gores for 11.
  - The fortress has nine dwarves at day 224. Two deaths take it back to its embark size.
- **Why the boar stays eight days.** Nothing makes it leave its home chunk, and nothing hunts it until the squad is armed.
  - The first kill order is on day 246. Under the fixture's T-387 policy, a squad without weapons is not sent against wild animals.
  - From then on, the soldiers kill both boars by day 250.8 and the third on day 270. The dwarves land 16 of 42 blows on the boars; the boars land 24 of 41 on the soldiers.
  - Three soldiers die to the boars.
- **Why the alert flaps.** The flapping is the fixture's T-387 policy, unchanged.
  - Because the squad is unarmed, a hearth alert raised for wild animals alone is lifted after half a day and not raised again for a day.
  - There are 49 alert changes in days 238 to 272.
  - The game raises nothing by itself.
- **Why 4 to 8 hits land in minutes.** Two faults in 10 keep a fleeing civilian within the boar's reach:
  - *The patient's trap.* A wounded dwarf whose thirst is critical flees. At its next selection, step 1's exception for a critical need skips `flee`, and step 3 rests the patient where it stands, beside the boar, waiting for a bandage. Then it flees again. One victim alternated six times in a quarter of a day and took eight hits.
  - *The run past the boar.* The fleeing civilian runs for the hearth hall even when the way passes the boar. 08 lets it go a few steps away, out of the boar's ground.
- **Correcting both faults does not save this year.** Measured as uncommitted patches:
  - Started on day 238 on master's timeline, the boars still kill every dwarf, whichever correction is applied.
  - Started on day 0, the "run out of the ground" rule leaves p0-2 with 15 dwarves. It does so by rerouting the year from the first boar on day 21: a dwarf dies to a boar on day 62, and the two day-196 deaths do not happen.
  - That rule also changes the hash of 18 of the 20 seeds. In one sample, dwarf deaths rise from 123 to 159 and the living fall from 907 to 816. Two seeds fail a T-156 witness when a wildfire burns their wooden gate walls.
  - The annual instrument improves by one dwarf: a badger death in forest-1 does not happen.
- **So the assertion changes, by T-334's rule.**
  - p0-2's "grew past embark size" has measured 12, 0, 15 and 0 under changes not aimed at it. It is now printed and not asserted (`GROWTH_FLIPS`).
  - The other six peril-0 seeds keep the assertion.
  - The two faults are a new row, to be taken on their own evidence. So are the burnt gate walls and three boars that never finish leaving the map.
