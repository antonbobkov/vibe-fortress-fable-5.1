## 1. Summary: how good is the UI now?

**Criterion 1, a clear UI a new player can read: partly met.** With mouse and keyboard together the game is playable and mostly readable. Refusals name the thing and the reason, panels show their keys, and hints arrive for food, drink, rooms and trade. Three things still fail a new player. The mouse alone cannot open a panel, pause or close anything. The keyboard alone cannot aim, and it places the wrong building. And the game says nothing about wild animals, which are what kills an unprepared fortress.

**Criterion 2, repetitive tasks are easy: mostly met.** Drag-to-place, the order picker with a typed filter and quantity, `r` to repeat an order, mark-and-apply in the labor manager, and stockpile presets all work and are short. The weak spots are finding an idle dwarf (the activity column is four characters wide), turning one labor on (25 key presses), and the order picker choosing the wrong recipe for the obvious word.

**Criterion 3, a clear message when something cannot be done: mostly met for the map, weak in panels.** Designations, placements and trade refusals are good. About a dozen keys still do nothing and say nothing, zones are made and trimmed without a word, and an order for something no workshop can make is accepted as if all were well.

**Under abuse the UI holds.** 177 steps of rapid input, resizes during drags, extreme window sizes, hostile text and nested modal screens produced no console error, no stuck state and no lost input. The same commands through the UI and through the hook give the same world hash.

### 1. The keyboard cannot aim, and building by keyboard places the wrong thing

**Blocks a keyboard-only player. Observed** (2/62 to 2/66, 2/74 to 2/77, 2/94, 3/15, and my replay).

- There is no tile cursor for tools. `Enter` acts on the tile at the centre of the map. With a panel open the camera stops at x = 0, so no tile left of x = 22 can be the centre (2/94, `94-probe-left-edge.png`). The wagon is at x = 1. In the reference fortress the stockpiles are on rows 3 to 9 and the centre cannot go above y = 22, so a stockpile could not be selected at all: "There is no zone at the cursor. Centre the map on a zone and press Enter." (3/15, `15-t2-kb-enter.png`).
- In Build, the category key arms the first row at once: `b`, `w` shows "Bowyer's workshop · click to place" (2/62). `Enter` does not pick the row. It places the building at the map centre immediately. In the capture the first `Enter` placed a carpenter's workshop where the player was not looking (2/64, `64-carpenter-tool.png`).
- Up and Down move the list while Left and Right pan the map. So "pan up one tile" changed the armed building from Carpenter's workshop to Butcher's shop, and the next `Enter` placed a butcher's shop (2/65, 2/66, `66-carpenter-placed.png`). The same thing turned a Door into a Coffin (2/77, `77-door-refused.png`). My replay confirms it: after `ArrowUp` the camera had not moved and the tool had changed.
- No ghost is drawn at the map centre. The panel says "Ghost follows the mouse" (2/63).
- A farm plot's crop cannot be chosen: the four season rows have no key (2/52 to 2/60, `52-farm-editor.png`). A keyboard-only fortress cannot farm.
- The Calendar has no key (2/28). A squad's kill order needs a click on the creature (3/33 to 3/35).

**What should happen.** A visible keyboard cursor. When a tool is armed, the arrow keys move the cursor (Shift moves ten), the camera follows it, the ghost and the tile summary are drawn at it, and `Enter` acts there. In Build, the category key opens the list without arming anything; Up, Down and `Enter` choose a row; only then is the tool armed and the arrows move the cursor. Filed as T-271 and T-272.

### 2. The mouse cannot open a panel, pause, close a panel or scroll a list

**Blocks a mouse-only player. Observed** (1/27 to 1/34, 1/79 to 1/81, 1/108 to 1/117, and my replay).

- The hotkey strip looks like buttons (`[d] dig  [b] build …`) and is not: clicking `[d] dig`, `[b] build` and `[?] help` did nothing (1/27 to 1/29, `27-strip-dig.png`). Clicking the population, mood, weather, level and speed fields did nothing (1/30 to 1/34, 1/117, `117-probe-speed-glyph.png`). Only the date opens a panel.
- Nothing closes a panel. The `[d]` in the title row is not a button (1/112).
- The game cannot be paused, resumed or sped up, and the game menu cannot be opened.
- The wheel over a list changes the map level under the sidebar instead of scrolling the list. In my replay the level went from 32 to 31 and the order picker did not move. The scrollbar is not a control (1/80, 1/81, `80-orders-picker-scrollbar.png`). Recipes below the first screen cannot be reached by mouse.
- "Priority: 5 (1-9 to change)", `[Ctrl+Z] undo last` and `[Del] clear` are not clickable (1/108 to 1/110), while the row under them is (1/111).

Everything inside an open panel works by mouse: rows, `[+]`, dropdowns, drags. The labor manager's cells toggle on a click (my replay). The gap is the frame around the panels.

**What should happen.** Every bracketed caption on the screen is a button. The strip opens panels, the title row has a close button, the speed glyph pauses, the status fields open their panels, the wheel scrolls the list under the pointer, and there is a visible menu button. Filed as T-270.

### 3. Nothing tells a new player that wild animals kill, or what stops them

The full answer is in section 8 (b). No hint spoke of danger. The Threats panel read "Active: none" with a bear on the map (`22-Y-threats.png`). The one hint about the alert and locked doors fires on a raid warning only. Filed as T-273.

### 4. Zones are made with no message, are almost invisible, and are trimmed silently

**Costs time and causes mistakes. Observed** (1/53, 1/63, 5/38, 5/40, 6/07, and my replay); **read** (`src/render/constants.ts:27`).

- After dragging a stockpile or a farm plot the status row still says "Stockpile · drag a rectangle · [Esc] cancel" and the log is unchanged (1/53, `53-stockpile-dragged.png`). Nothing says a zone now exists. Designations and buildings do say so ("Bed x5 placed", 4/67).
- The zone is a 25% tint over a black cell background, so it is a faint dark patch. In `63-farm-dragged.png` a stockpile and a farm plot are on screen and I could find neither without the dump. In [`zone-tints-after-overlap.png`](2026-10-12-ui-ux/replay/zone-tints-after-overlap.png) the selected farm plot is the barely visible rectangle at the top left. The selected zone is not outlined. Both palettes look the same here (6/07, 6/12).
- A farm plot dragged over part of a stockpile was made from the free tiles only. In my replay a 15-tile drag gave "Farm plot #1 (9 t)" with no message; the capture's 5/38 and 5/40 show the same silence. A drag with no free tile does explain itself ("Bedroom: 0 of 4 tiles qualify…", 5/39).
- The zone type list cuts its own labels: "Dining ha", "Meeting a" (1/63).

**What should happen.** "Farm plot #1 made: 9 tiles (6 left out: they belong to Stockpile #1)". Zones drawn so that they can be seen, with an outline on the selected one. Filed as T-274 (after T-245, which changes the same drawing code).

### 5. An order that cannot be made is accepted without a warning; the picker picks the wrong recipe

**Costs time and causes mistakes. Observed** (5/47, 5/50, 3/44 to 3/48, 9/110, 9/114).

- "Ordered: x1 Mail shirt · any forge" with no forge built (5/47, `47-O1-no-workshop.png`). The reason shows only later, in the Orders list ("x1 Mail shirt · no forge") and, with the order selected, at the foot of the panel ("No forge is built: [b] build ? Workshops."). The Jobs panel never lists the order (3/46, `46-B-jobs.png`), so a player who looks for "why is nothing happening" under Jobs finds nothing.
- Typing "barrel" highlights "Metal barrel" first, so `Enter` orders a metal barrel at a forge. The wooden barrel every new fortress needs is second (4/51, 2/70, 5/50).
- `Shift+N`, `Enter` with an empty Find orders the first recipe in the list: "Ordered: x1 Arrowheads · any forge" (9/110, 9/114).
- A quantity of 0 becomes 1 without a word (5/50, `50-O4-quantity-zero.png`).

**What should happen.** The order is still accepted, but the status row says "Ordered: x1 Mail shirt. No forge is built yet: [b] Build › Workshops." Blocked orders appear in Jobs under their own heading with the reason. A recipe that a built workshop can make ranks above one that none can. `Enter` on an empty Find does nothing. Filed as T-276.

## 6. Legibility

- **Glyphs and colours.** The map, the dwarves and the panels read well at 1280x800 and 960x540. Alerts in orange with `!`, hints in grey with `☼`, key captions in yellow: this is consistent everywhere. The hint line is dark grey on black and is the faintest text on the screen, though it is the line a new player most needs (`19-fortress-open.png`). **Observed.**
- **Both palettes.** Nothing in the captured screens relies on a colour pair that the colourblind palette fails to separate (6/06 to 6/15). The zone tints are too faint in both (issue 4).
- **Window sizes.** 960x540 gives the minimum 80x45 grid at 12-pixel cells and stays readable (`31-B-designate-960x540.png`). Below that the canvas is scaled and stays usable. 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 are fine. At 3840x2160 the cells are small and the map is a quarter of the window (issue 10).
- **80 columns.** The default window is exactly the minimum width, so every over-long line is cut there: the embark header and footer and the hotkey strip (T-244, open), the first hint, three refusals in the table above, the two-column panel labels and Help.
- **Long names.** The inputs stop at their limits (24 and 32). A 24-character name is cut at 15 in Units and at 10 in the labor grid, with the full name in the tooltip and in Look (6/49 to 6/56). Acceptable.
- **A full log.** The log strip and the Log panel stay readable when full, with `[1] All [2] Alerts`. Lines have no date (issue 9).
- **The reference fortress** is a wall of furniture glyphs (`07-t1-units.png`). That is the fixture, not the game.

## 7. What is still missing

**Commands with no UI, or with half a UI.**
- By mouse: open a panel, close a panel, pause, speed, the game menu, scroll a list, set a designation priority (issue 2).
- By keyboard: aim at a tile, choose a crop, open the Calendar, pick a kill target, anything that is hover-only (tile summary, tooltips on cut text) (issue 1).
- Save has no name prompt and no confirmation; the Load list is the only place a save can be seen.

**Information a player needs and cannot see.**
- That a predator is on the map and is a danger (section 8 b).
- Whether a wounded dwarf will be treated, and what is missing (issue 7).
- Why an order is not made, from the Jobs panel (issue 5).
- Where a zone is (issue 4).
- When a log line happened (issue 9).
- Who is skilled at a labor, from the labor manager (issue 6).
- What `+1` and `!4` in the status row mean.
- That food is running out. Flow 7 went from no warning straight to "! Nori Bearhold is starving." on day 54. There is a hint for drink (`hint_still`) and none for food.

**What a Dwarf Fortress player expects and does not find.**
- A keyboard cursor for designating and building.
- Skill levels in the labor screen.
- A health screen: who is hurt, how badly, who treats them.
- A list of stockpiles and zones to jump to.
- Announcements with a date, and a click or key on a line to go to where it happened. (The spec has the log centre the map on danger; the capture did not test it.)
- A help text that explains how to begin, not only a key list.

## 8. Answers to the questions handed to this audit

### (a) The magma kiln (from T-194)

**Hiding it is right.** A workshop with no recipes is a trap: the player spends magma-safe blocks on a building that can do nothing. The build menu does not list it (8 `B-category-*`), the order picker finds nothing for "kiln", "glass", "ceramic", "brick" or "pottery" (8 `D-picker-filter-*`), and Help does not name it. **Observed.**

**Nothing a player can see still mentions it. Read:** the only strings are the content row (`src/content/buildings.json:905`) and the spec, which says it is reserved (`docs/03-content/workshops-and-recipes.md:36`). Two small things remain. The menu hides it by naming its id in code (`src/ui/panels/build.ts:41`), against the rule that content ids do not appear in code; the rule should be "a workshop with no recipes is not listed". And the capture could not place one even through the hook, so nothing tests that a kiln in an old save does no harm. Filed as T-284 (small).

### (b) Danger guidance (from T-220 and the wildlife work)

**Does the game tell a new player before it matters? No.**

What an unprepared player saw in 70 days (flow 7; **observed**):

- Day 8: "A lynx has arrived." Day 13: "A fox has arrived." Day 26: "A badger has arrived." Day 27: "A boar has arrived. x2". Day 63: "A bear has arrived." All are plain notes, the same as "A deer has arrived. x2". No `!`, no pause, no hint.
- The hints that did appear were about rooms (day 6), the hospital (day 8), winter (day 20), seeds (day 22) and the depot (day 42). None is about defence.
- The simulation raised `ThreatNearby` on day 32. Nothing was shown for it.
- At day 71 the Military panel read "Alert: Calm … No squads yet: [+] makes one." and the Threats panel read "Peril 0 · Renown 3.3 … Active: none … Thieves from Spring, Y2", with a bear on the map (`21-Y-military.png`, `22-Y-threats.png`). The Animals panel has a Wild tab; nothing points to it.
- The embark screen's only advice is "Hint: two picks and one axe are the minimum for a working start." The region panel showed "Peril 0", which a new player reads as "safe".

**Read in code:** `hint_alert`, the only text that explains "Lock on alert" and the Hearth alert, fires on the first `ThreatWarning`, and only the raid scheduler emits that event (`src/systems/threats/index.ts:362`). `hint_gate` fires after the first raid has ended, and `hint_troll` after the first door is broken. Every defensive hint arrives after the event it is about. Wild animals are announced by one string for all species (`announce.animal_spawned`, severity note).

**What it should say, where and when.** The measured rule is simple: clubs for a squad, a hearth shelter dug into rock, and a door on it set to lock on alert, all in the first two weeks, and almost nobody dies. The game should say exactly that, three times, each time earlier than the danger.

| Where | When | Text |
|---|---|---|
| Embark screen, hint line (it alternates with the existing hint) | always | "Hint: wolves and bears come in the first season. Bring an axe; clubs for a squad can be made from logs." |
| World map, region panel, under Peril | always | "Peril counts raiders. Wild beasts live everywhere." |
| A new hint, `hint_shelter` | day 3 of the first spring, if no squad exists | "Wild beasts will come. Dig a hall into rock, put a door on it, click the door and choose Lock on alert. Make a squad in [m] and order clubs at a carpenter's workshop." |
| The arrival line, for a species that attacks dwarves | when it arrives | "! A bear has come to the valley." as an alert (orange, in the Alerts tab), with the log's jump to its tile. Deer and other harmless animals keep the plain note. |
| A new hint, `hint_predator` | the first such arrival | "A beast that hunts dwarves is near. [m] then [3] raises the Hearth alert: civilians go to the hearth and doors set to Lock on alert shut. [n] › Wild shows where it is." |
| Threats panel | always | A "Wildlife" section above Caverns: one row for each species on the map that attacks dwarves, with the count and "near the hall" or "far". "Active: none" must not be shown while a row is there. |
| Military panel, with no squad | always | Under "No squads yet": "A squad of four with clubs holds off wolves." |
| Military panel, the alert row | always | One line under the three alerts that says what the current one does: "Hearth: civilians go to the hearth; doors set to Lock on alert are shut." |
| The existing `hint_alert` | also on the first dwarf wounded by an animal | unchanged text |

The first predator sighting should also pause the game once, as the first raid does, so that the hint is read. Filed as T-273. The wording about "the first season" and "four with clubs" should be checked against the measurements in [`../../docs/balance-status.md`](../../docs/balance-status.md) when the task is done.

### (c) The UI under abuse (handed over from T-115)

**Ruling: it passes.** 177 steps, no console error, warning or exception (9, all steps). **Observed.**

- **Rapid input.** Fifty keys in one task in each of seven contexts left the game answering keys, on the screen expected (9/01 to 9/10).
- **Resize during a drag.** The drag survives, the count follows the pointer and the release places what the status row says: "Bed x3 placed" (9/15). A designation drag released after a resize is dropped without harm (9/18).
- **Extreme sizes.** All 19 panels and the game menu draw at 640x360 and 3840x2160. Only Designate has text in the last column, at every size (issue 10).
- **Text fields.** Length limits hold. Emoji and right-to-left text are counted and stored without damage. Two defects: control characters (bell, NUL, ESC, DEL, tab, CR, backspace) are accepted as a name, and the Units list then shows "???????" (9/73, 9/74); and the Units filter keeps its text when the panel is reopened (9/83 to 9/90), which hides most dwarves until the player notices.
- **Modal screens.** From seven modal screens, global keys never opened a second screen on top, and `Esc` always went back one level.
- **Determinism.** Ten commands through the real UI and the same ten through the hook, on the same ticks: world hash `2a52bc0bf5bfd8e2` at tick 1000 in all three games (9/171 to 9/177). The UI adds nothing to the simulation.
- **One thing to watch.** In Stocks, a burst that included `Space` left the clock running (9/09). The capture did not record whether the filter field had focus. If it did, a space typed into a filter resumes the game. **Not established.**

Filed as T-282 (control characters, the sticky filter, and the Space question).

### (d) The work file's own questions

- **Are the `[S-x]` captions readable in two-column panels?** The captions are. The labels beside them are not always: four of fourteen in Designate and two of fourteen in Zones are cut, with no mark (issue 10). `S-` itself is explained only on page 2 of Help, as "Shift+letter  a panel action on a panel letter: [S-m]", and Help writes `Shift+N` where panels write `[S-n]`. One line at the foot of every panel that uses the form ("S- is Shift") would settle it.
- **Does two presses of `Esc` to leave Designate bother a player?** No. The first cancels the tool and the second closes the panel, and the status row says so ("[Esc] cancel"). The trouble is the third press: it opens the game menu (4/123, and it happened to me in the replay). Right click already cancels the tool without closing anything. Keep the two presses. Do not open the game menu from an `Esc` that follows another within half a second.
- **Is "new order" on `Shift+N` discoverable?** Yes. The empty Orders panel says "No work orders. [S-n] adds one, or click a workshop on the map." and its first row, "[S-n] new order", is a button (4/49, 1/78). What is not discoverable is Orders itself: `[o]` is not in the hotkey strip (T-244, open).
