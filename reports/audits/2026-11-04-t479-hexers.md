# Measurement: Gloam hexers in raids and sieges

**Task:** T-479 · **Date:** 2026-11-04 (runs of 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-04) · **Commit examined:** the T-479 branch, `659cc62e` (master `37da07a4` merged: T-476, T-484) for the twenty-seed file; `82bc5fda` and `9af8f64c` for the peril-4 probes · **Kind:** measurement · **Source:** [the T-479 work file](../../docs/work/T-479-runecraft-hexers.md), [25 Runecraft](../../docs/02-systems/25-runecraft.md) § Hexers

## The answer

- **Hexers come where the rule says and nowhere else.** One in a raid and two in a siege, at peril 3 from year 3 and at peril 4 from year 2. At peril 2 and below nothing new is planned or drawn: a peril-2 raid in year 3 plans the same members with the same rng state as in year 1 (`t479-hexers.test.ts`), and `pp_defence` (peril 0) gives a record equal as JSON to T-475's and T-476's: end tick 30,440, 11,724 events, the same 22 combat events and the same state in all 21 streams (`2026-11-02-t479-pp-defence.json`), on the branch before and after the merge of T-476.
- **A hexer does what the specification says.** In a cell it casts exactly its 20 frost bolts at its target, says nothing when they are gone (no `RuneSpent`, no vial), and once the cell opens walks up and clubs with the wand. It holds its bolt while a Gloam archer stands on its line, does not move or spend, and throws as soon as the line is clear. Its wand is foreign on its corpse, becomes stock after `strip_corpse`, and only a squad member with `runecraft` takes it (`test/unit/runecraft/t479-hexers.test.ts`).
- **In a fight a hexer is a modest addition.** Against six Novice soldiers in full iron kit in the open, a peril-4 raid of ten wins 23 of 24 fights with no hexer, 21 with one and 21 with two. The hexer casts in 9 fights of 24, 3.3 bolts a fight, and hits a soldier 1.0 times for 2.2 damage; it spends a third of its rune at most. It never hit its own side. It waits most of the fight: warriors stand on its line.
- **At peril 4 the fortress does worse with hexers, and most of it is the year-2 siege of one seed.** Five seeds, the same code with and without hexers: **86** dwarf deaths and **132** living at year 3 with hexers, **77** and **191** without; two fortresses extinct against one. Over the five seeds 29 hexers came and cast 50 bolts; 25 hit a dwarf; one dwarf was killed by a hexer. Four seeds end within a few dwarves of their pair or better (45 / 50, 0 / 0, 29 / 42, 58 / 47). The fifth, `three-years-p4-2`, lost its fortress on day 176, two days after a siege of 26 with two hexers who cast 36 bolts there; the siege's captains did most of the killing (13 of 22 deaths). Without hexers the same siege leaves it with 52 dwarves at year 3.
- **At peril 3 hexers come in year 3 and cast nothing** in two seeds: 8 came, every party looted outside the gate; 20 deaths and 44 living with them, 15 and 45 without.
- **The set survival rule holds.** Twenty seeds at the end: alive at year 1 **19 of 19** counted (one seed stopped by an invariant, below), peril 0 grown **7 of 7**, peril-0 seeds with a hunger or thirst death **0 of 7**. Hexers cannot reach year 1 at any peril.
- **Two seeds of the file fail on their own, neither by a hexer.** `three-years-p4-4` stops on day 251 on the T-176 invariant `job.unreachable-with-path` (a plant job whose fetch walk failed is never retried; filed). `three-years-p2-7` fails its T-156 witness "a dining hall" after writing its figures; peril 2 has no hexer and draws nothing new, so this is master's (it carries T-476 and T-484; filed: [p2-7 dining hall](../../docs/work/T-NEW-p2-7-dining-hall-witness.md)).

### What moved the peril-4 seeds

The first version of the kit rolled each row on 18's stream, as other kits do. Those four draws a hexer moved every later roll of the schedule, and the seeds parted for reasons that had nothing to do with frost: `three-years-p4-6` met an ambush on day 317 that does not come in its pair, and lost 68 dwarves to it in five days, every one to the ambush party in `engage` (filed: [ambush massacre](../../docs/work/T-NEW-ambush-massacre.md)). With that version the five seeds read 157 deaths and 131 living. The kit was then made fixed, as above, so that the measurement shows the hexers and not a reshuffled schedule.

### The invariant in three-years-p4-4

With hexers, `three-years-p4-4` stops on day 251 on `job.unreachable-with-path`: job 4988, `plant:74899`, a surface plot tile, blocked `unreachable` with `failedWalk` for a day although all 28 workers have a reachable stand tile, the alert calm and no party on the map. It is the class filed before for patient jobs; hexers only change the timeline that reaches it (filed: [failed fetch walk](../../docs/work/T-NEW-failed-fetch-walk-never-retried.md)).

## Verdict

Hexers make peril 4 harder from year 2, by a fortress in five seeds, and the player can read it: a red `G` named "gloam hexer" that throws frost bolts from eight tiles, slows what it wounds, and clubs once its twenty bolts are gone; its look panel shows its rune and "never full". The set survival rule holds, so no rule or number was changed. Every number of § Hexers stays as it was; `HEXER_RUNE` 20 is measured here.
