# Audit: a new player's first hour

**Task:** T-605 · **Date:** 2026-11-18 · **Commit examined:** master `281fa710`, and the T-605 branch for the fixes · **Kind:** a UI and UX pass played as a new player, with the small fixes · **Source:** [the T-605 work file](../../docs/work/T-605-first-hour-ux.md)

## The answer

- **Three things stop a new fortress without telling the player why.** A new stockpile takes nothing and its inspector called that "Custom" (fixed); a stair column whose top tile is skipped, or a dig box that touches the rock only at a corner, waits for ever with "no path" in the Jobs panel (filed); and the hints that explain the dangers of the first season are longer than a line and cannot be read in full anywhere (filed).
- **Zones answer late.** A zone made while paused gives no word and no inspector until the next tick, so a player draws it again and is told no tile qualifies; and after a zone is made the tool keeps `Enter` while the inspector says "Enter choose crop" (filed).
- **The map is under-explained.** The help legend lacks the tree glyphs and the outdoor grass and flowers that cover the first screen, lists `*` only as a web, and the hover line calls a grassy tile "Clay floor" (filed). The world map's cursor is nearly invisible on forest and snow (filed).
- **Eight small ones are fixed here:** raw decimals in the Mood and Population panels, `fiber|straw|reed` in waiting reasons, content ids in shrub names (`Wild_nettle shrub`), the Help start page advising clubs where every other line says iron spears, a cut `[?] he»` in the status row, a clipped zones help line, a black band where the sidebar was, and the empty stockpile.

## How it was examined

A Vite dev server in test mode and a headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 (80 × 50 cells), driven by a small Playwright script through the real keyboard and mouse, as the e2e tests drive it; the screen's text came from the test hook and the pictures from Playwright. The run: a new standard world, the world map and its filter, a peril-0 temperate forest with a river and a lake (Oldford), the Balanced loadout, then the first 18 days of spring: help, look and hover, a dug room, a stair shaft, chopping trees, a carpenter's workshop and a still, two stockpiles, a farm plot, a dining hall, a bed order, the units, jobs, labors, military and mood panels, the log, a quick save, a menu save and a load. Time was passed with the speed keys and, for whole days, the hook's `step`. The screenshots (named below, `NN-name.png`) were kept outside the repository.

Severity: **blocks** (the player cannot get the thing done without knowledge the game does not give), **confuses** (the player is misled or stuck for a while), **polish**.

## What changed on the branch

Findings 1, 10–16 are fixed, with their tests in `test/unit/ui/t605-first-hour.test.ts`:

- **Empty stockpile:** the preset header reads "Nothing" for an empty filter and "Takes nothing: choose a Preset." follows in the alert colour; the log names it "Stockpile #1 (Nothing)"; Help's start page says to choose a preset.
- **Plant names:** `terrain.inspect` gives a tree, sapling or shrub tile its species' name ("Wild nettle shrub").
- **Mood and Population:** the hall spirit and every thought weight are whole numbers.
- **Waiting reasons:** an input with alternatives is named "fiber or straw or reed" and every alternative is counted.
- **Help:** the start page's squad has iron spears.
- **Status row 1:** after `[Esc] menu` → `[Esc]`, `[?] help` shortens to `[?]` rather than being cut.
- **Zones help line:** "Usable tiles show ▒, others x."
- **Camera:** a frame whose map view is wider than the last one's clamps the camera again, so closing a panel never shows past the map's edge.
