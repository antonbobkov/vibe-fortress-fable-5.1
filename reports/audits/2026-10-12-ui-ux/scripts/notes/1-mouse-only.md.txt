Method: Playwright mouse events only (click, right-click, drag, wheel, middle drag), starting at the main menu with the default seed, default party and default supplies. World: standard size, region "Bitterbrae" (tundra, wealth 2, peril 0, temper 3), reached by clicking a land cell. Where the mouse had no way to do the next thing, the step records the attempt and the next step uses one key press, marked `[KEY - ...]`; from there the run is mouse plus those keys. Map positions for placements were chosen with the hook's readers (setup only, not an input): free 3x3 patches near the wagon.

Mouse could do (observed): every main-menu row; "[g] Generate" and "[Enter] Continue" on the world screen; choose a world-map cell; region panel buttons `[f] filter`, `[f] close`, `[?] legend`, `[?] close`, `[Enter] Embark here`; embark tabs, a dwarf row, `[p]`, `[Enter] Embark`; click a tile (Look), a dwarf, a building (building panel), the date (Calendar panel); right click (Look, or cancel a tool); wheel (z level); middle drag (pan); Shift+wheel; Ctrl+wheel down (zoom out); rows and buttons inside an open panel (Dig, Chop tree, Stairwell, Stockpile, Farm plot, Meeting area, Bedroom, category rows, workshop and furniture rows, `[S-n] new order`, a recipe row, `[Enter] add`, the stockpile preset header and options, `[+]` buttons, crop dropdowns); drag a rectangle for a designation, a zone and a building; the game menu's "Rename fortress" row.

Mouse could not do (observed, each tried): open any fortress panel other than Calendar (by date), Look (by tile) and Building (by building) - the hotkey strip in status row 2, `[?] help`, the population, mood, weather, z-level and speed fields were clicked and nothing opened; Designate, Build, Zones, Orders, Units, Jobs, Labors, Stocks, Military, Animals, Threats, Civilizations, Population, Mood, Log, Overlays, Help and Debug were opened by key; open the game menu (Esc); pause, resume or change speed (the speed glyph was clicked, no change; Space was used); close a panel (the `[d]` in the title row was clicked, no change; the sidebar stayed until Esc was pressed); scroll the order picker's recipe list (wheel over the sidebar changed the z level instead; a click on the scrollbar track and a drag of its thumb changed nothing; only recipes in the visible part were reachable); type a filter or a quantity; set a priority 1-9 (the "Priority: 5" row was clicked, no change); `[Ctrl+Z] undo last` and `[Del] clear` rows clicked, no visible change; Ctrl+wheel up at zoom 1 (no visible change).
