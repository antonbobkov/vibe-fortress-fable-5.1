Fortresses used. The harness offers `test/fixtures/reference-fortress.deepholm` (the 80-dwarf reference fortress) and `test/fixtures/played-fortress.deepholm` (20 dwarves, played 4,200 ticks by a script). Both were loaded through the real screens: main menu `l` (Load), then the import key `i`, then `Enter` on the imported save. The `i` press had to go through the hook (`h.key('i')`), because a real key press did not raise the browser's file chooser in this harness (the existing `export-import` spec also uses the hook for it). The caravan for the trade task was brought with the hook, as the task allows ("only for state").

What the reference fortress contains (read through the hook's readers): 80 dwarves; 99 beds, 100 chairs, 100 tables, 100 statues, 100 chain posts, 100 paved floors, one wagon; 15 stockpile zones (4 tiles each, on rows y=3..9 of level z27) and 15 bedroom zones; no workshop, no trade depot, no farm plot, no squad. It opens paused on Day 1 of Spring, Year 3 (tick 269201), world hash `2d9920c34c319d0e`. After stepping 1,200 ticks (hook, setup) the log shows "A raid has arrived! x2", "Hrolf Brighteye has lost consciousness." and later "The raid has ended.", the status row shows `+1` patient and `!4` hostiles, the Threats panel lists "Active: bandits (4, engage)", and a Hillfolk caravan party is in state `waiting` although there is no depot. In the labors grid every labor of every dwarf visible was on (all `■`). The played fortress has carpenter, craftsdwarf, kitchen, mason, mechanic and still workshops, one squad ("Gate", 4 members), one farm plot, two stockpiles, no forge, no depot.

Setup done by the hook, not by the UI (and so not counted): `SetLabor mining off` for the first dwarf (task 1, so that there is a labor to give); stepping 1,200 ticks (reference fortress) or 600 ticks (played fortress); for the trade task a finished trade depot, a request, 20 easy meals moved into the depot, the first dwarf given the broker title, stepping until the caravan was at the depot, and stepping tick by tick until the trade session opened.
