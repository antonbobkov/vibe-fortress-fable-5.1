# Measurement: a warded squad in the combat-pace arena, and no-channel combat unchanged

**Task:** T-473 · **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Commit examined:** master `83f5f311` with the T-473 branch on it · **Kind:** measurement; no constant, formula or content value was changed by it · **Source:** [the T-473 work file](../../docs/archive/tasks/T-473-runecraft-remaining-spells.md), [the T-470 caster arena](2026-11-02-t470-caster-arena.md)

## The answer

- **A warded squad of six loses less blood than an unwarded one, at the measured peril-2 raid and at the ambush.** Against a peril-2 raid of ten in the open, five Novice soldiers in full kit and a Proficient runecaster with a common iron staff of warding lose **2,156** blood a fight between them at the end of the fight (the dead counted at their whole 612), against **3,211** when the same caster casts nothing; the soldiers alone **2,099** against **2,690**. The warded squad drives the raid off in **24 of 24** fights, the unwarded in 14 (10 squads down); soldiers dead a day later **3.46** against **4.63**, and the caster dies in 2 fights against 23. Against a peril-2 ambush of four the warded soldiers lose **27** blood a fight against **383**, and **0.04** of them are dead a day later against **0.63**.
- **The ward lapses on time.** A wand's ward is 4 armour for 420 ticks and an iron staff's 7 for 720; both lapse at the tick and the caster wards again then (`test/unit/runecraft/t473-remaining-spells.test.ts`). In the open arena the caster wards 1.9 times a fight and 5.7 creatures a fight are warded; in the ambush 1.6 times and 9.7.
- **The ward does its work by armour, not by numbers.** The raiders land about as many hits on the soldiers either way (98 a fight warded, 95 unwarded, in the open), but a warded soldier takes **0.57** a hit (819 hits for 467) against 4.8 a hit unwarded.
- **Combat with no channel in play is unchanged, draw for draw.** `pp_defence` on its seed gives a record equal in every field to the T-470 one (`2026-11-02-t470-pp-defence-after.json`): end tick 30,440, the same 22 combat events byte for byte, 11,724 events in all, and the same state in all 21 rng streams. The six no-channel arena digests pinned in `t470-no-channel-equivalence.test.ts` still match, and no golden moved.

### No-channel equivalence

`pp_defence` was run by the T-470 probe (`reports/data/2026-11-02-t470-pp-defence.probe.ts.txt`, writing `…-t473-pp-defence.json`) and compared with `…-t470-pp-defence-after.json`: equal as JSON. The ward is added in `armorReduction` only from an unexpired `ward` effect, flight is read only from an unexpired `flee` effect, and every casting call (`drinkVial`, `castSupport`, the hold distance) is made only for a channel holder; with no `Spellbound` and no channel nothing is looked up that changes a result, and nothing draws.

## What this does not measure

- Mend, Dread, Firebloom and Daze in a squad fight: their rules are pinned by tests (who is mended, at most four sent running and back, the party's retreat count unmoved, no bloom into a square with one of the caster's own, a bloom catching who walks in, mental immunity at size 180 and for the deep), not measured for balance.
- Vials over a campaign: a caster drinks at most three a day (pinned by a test).
- A caster in a ranged squad, behind a fortification, or against the trollkin or the wyrm.
