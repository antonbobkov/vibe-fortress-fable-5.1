# Playtest 2026-11-25 (fourth): peril 4, raids, a siege, the cistern and a fall

**What:** the three-year script's fortress at **peril 4** on three new seeds, run headless into Years 2 and 3, then played in stretches in the browser from its saves. **Commit:** `ee23858c` (master, after T-735/T-742). **How:** [drive.test.ts.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-4/drive.test.ts.txt) runs `threeYearsWorld` + `setupThreeYearsFortress` (Balanced, a 48 × 48 temperate forest with a brook, wealth 2) on `playtest-1125-p4`, `-p4b` and `-p4c`. It adds my orders to the script: the cistern and well begun on day 30, which T-663's script otherwise starts only when drink runs short, and a 5 × 3 ward with six beds and a chest from day 50. It saves on fixed days and on every raid or siege warning. Two plain runs, the script alone (`PT_PLAIN=1`), are the control. The cistern's lever was played on `bd_cistern_well`'s fortress, saved at its breach and at its working well ([cistern.test.ts.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-4/cistern.test.ts.txt)). Saves were imported into `vite --mode test` in headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 (80 columns), driven by Playwright with the keyboard and mouse, and stepped with `window.__deepholm.step`. From each load on, I made the decisions. Populations and events are in [events.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-4/events.txt). The save of the siege's eve, Autumn 12, Year 2, is [deepholm-p4-y2-siege.bin](2026-11-25-playtest-4/deepholm-p4-y2-siege.bin). Screenshots are in [2026-11-25-playtest-4/](2026-11-25-playtest-4/). No console errors or page errors.

**In short.** Peril 4 is brutal. With my orders, `p4` peaked at 11 dwarves and ended Year 3 with 2, `p4b` was empty by day 168, and `p4c` ended with 2. Plain, `p4` reached 18 by day 196 and `p4b` lost 9 of 11 to its first ambush on day 65. The killers were the first Gloam ambush (days 64 to 65), wolves (5 dwarves in Summer, Year 1; 3 more, the Thane among them, in Spring, Year 3), and raids of 11 to 14 every three to four weeks. A siege of 20 (27 on the map) came on day 181. I played four threats the way the game teaches, Hearth alert and doors locked: the raid of Autumn 20, Year 1, the siege, a raid and an ambush on `p4b`, and a raid on `p4c`. No one died in any of them. Three of the four times the whole fortress went to thirst 100; the fourth fortress kept its drink inside. I also met a raid of 11 on Calm with three soldiers active; all seven dwarves were dead in two days and the legend opened. The script's cistern never finished at 6 to 9 dwarves (stuck at its lever for 200 days), so I played the floodgate on `bd_cistern_well`'s finished well.
