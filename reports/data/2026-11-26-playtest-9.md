## In short

The builder's tools read well: the room inspector, the Mood panel's "Helps:" lines and the Thane's line in Units. But the builder's goals are out of reach, and the screen does not say why.

1. **No masterwork in any fortress.** It needs level 13, 5,980 experience. Craft skills train on stone, and stone is scarce. A dug rock tile drops a stone three times in ten, and the mason's standing orders take it first. Emberdelve's dedicated crafter went from 5 to 8 in 364 days. Playtest 7's best crafter was 8 after six years. Mining, which needs no input, reached 12 to 16. The workshop meanwhile advised "dig down to rock: the soil here is … levels deep".
2. **Statues barely come.** A statue takes three stones at once. The crafter takes each stone as it is dug, so the statue order waited 253 days for its first statue. In the browser it came only once I suspended the crafter.
3. **Nothing explains quality.** No hint, no help line and no skill line says that quality comes from skill, or that masterwork starts at 13. The workshop's [w] steps through dwarves blind.
4. **Rooms matter, but the next tier is a guess.** Two doors took the hall from 184 to 276 (×1.5, shown nowhere). A statue adds about 7. Grand needs 400. Every hearth hall in playtests 7, 8 and 9 ended Fine.
5. **The Thane wants only two things:** deepfire or fine meals, 17 and 6 times in playtest 7's 23 seasons. The fortress drinks the deepfire before the season ends.
6. **Nothing goes characterfully wrong.** There was one sulk in 364 days (day 14), and none in playtest 7's six years. Every sulk counter read 0. By Year 4, Emberdelve had 78 dwarves and 17 bedrooms ("bored ×72, no_bedroom ×61"), and still nobody sulked.

The happiest outcome is reachable in a small fortress: 3 of 39 dwarves Ecstatic and hall spirit Content (66) on day 224. As it grew to 78 dwarves it fell to Fine (52), with nobody Ecstatic.
