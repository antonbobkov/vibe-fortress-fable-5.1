# Playtest 2026-11-26 (seventh): the late game and the deep, Years 4 to 6

**What:** one Balanced fortress at peril 2 taken from the start of Year 4 to the start of Year 7, looking at the caverns, runecraft, the titles and the Thane's whims, wealth, trade at scale, a population of 60 to 80 (moods, the hospital, burials), performance and the late threats. **Commit:** `8ba00747` (master, after T-772/T-781). **How:** [drive.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-7/drive.test.ts.txt) runs the three-year script's fortress (`threeYearsWorld` + `setupThreeYearsFortress`, Balanced, a 48 × 48 temperate forest with a brook, wealth 2) at **peril 2** on the new seed `playtest-1126-p2` ("Boarfell") for 672 days, to Day 1 of Spring, Year 7. It saves every 28 days from day 336 and at each warning. Its event log is [events.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-7/events.txt). The Year 4 save ([boarfell-y4-spring1.bin](2026-11-26-playtest-7/boarfell-y4-spring1.bin)) was imported into `vite --mode test` in headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 and driven by Playwright over CDP with the keyboard and mouse. I played it myself, without the script, from Spring, Year 4 to Summer, Year 5: I dug to cavern 2, raised and lowered the alert, buried the dead, traded with all three caravans, appointed titles, and started on runecraft. My fortress at Summer, Year 5 is [boarfell-browser-y5-summer.bin](2026-11-26-playtest-7/boarfell-browser-y5-summer.bin). The script's Year 7 save ([boarfell-y7-spring1.bin](2026-11-26-playtest-7/boarfell-y7-spring1.bin), 80 dwarves) was loaded for the screens at scale and for timing. Where deepsilver lies was read from the save with [deepsilver.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-7/deepsilver.test.ts.txt), and the cavern pockets with the hook. Screenshots are in [2026-11-26-playtest-7/](2026-11-26-playtest-7/). No page errors, apart from one my own event listener threw (fixed in the harness).

## In short

The game holds up at 80 dwarves: speed 4 keeps 80 ticks a second with a 17.6 ms median frame. The panels stay readable at that size. The late game is legible where earlier playtests fixed it (raid warnings, coffin lines, the squad's "Now" line). Where it is new it is not.

When I played it myself, Year 4 was hard and mostly fair. About 22 dwarves died in fifteen months, against the script's 8 in three years:
- an ambush at the depot killed three, the Thane among them;
- a raid of 17 met late killed eight;
- one blind stalker from the cavern I opened fought seventeen dwarves and the cat in the hall, and five bled out because the fortress had had no cloth for a year;
- a dwarf I walled into the cavern corridor starved;
- bandits and a second ambush killed three more.

Immigration refilled the fortress to 80 each time. What hurt was what went unsaid:
- the walled-in dwarf "is starving" for fourteen days beside a thousand meals, while the hint says to slaughter the cat;
- the Thane and the Broker died, and their titles emptied without a word;
- the breach line names no cavern, and the monster notes name no monster;
- the hospital shows "Requirements met" with one bed for eighty;
- burial is a chore of ordering and placing every coffin, and nine dead waited 82 days.

The deep was thin on this map:
- the three caverns are rock-sealed pockets, and cavern 1's thirteen monsters never left theirs in six years;
- deepsilver lies only in the Sea of Fire's rim, 25 levels below the hall;
- runecraft took a year to reach its second step: seeds came only from the next spring's caravan, and the jeweler's standing order cut the glowstone bought for the lamp.

Left to the script, Years 4 to 6 at peril 2 are the same year three times. The same raid of 16 comes each spring and loots a locked-in fortress. The Thane wishes for deepfire in 10 seasons of 12. Trades shrink year on year, and the population sits at the cap of 80. About half the fortress holds "I have nothing to do." There is no siege, masterwork, artifact or tantrum. Fair and safe, but dull.

Worked well at scale:
- **Performance.** At 78 to 80 dwarves (85 creatures, about 1,100 entities with a position, 1,400 to 1,700 items), speed 4 held 80.1 ticks a second (speed 1: 20.0, speed 2: 39.9). Frames: median 17.6 ms, 95th percentile 23.6 ms, worst 59 ms. The Debug panel's last tick was 2.2 ms, and `step(1200)` (a day) took 3.2 s. A year at speed 4 takes 28 minutes.
- **Readable panels at 80.** Units ("Units (80, 21 idle)"), Mood's "Thoughts held by most", Population's "Immigration paused by cap", Stocks' totals, Threats' party makeup ("9 warriors, 5 archers, a captain"), the Civilizations reputation log, and the labor manager's grid with all 27 labors.
- **Lines and panels.** The raid warning's band and day ("! A horde of Gloam raiders has been seen at the northern edge. They will come tomorrow.", T-772). The coffin line with its count ("only 1 coffin is free: build 6 more"). The squad's "Now: 2 at post · 1 on the way · 1 asleep · 2 on a break" (T-771). The "No shelter: 4 dwarves keep working" line. The bleeding line that names the fix. The trade-wait lines ("Eydis Oaktongue, the Broker, has the trading labor off: another dwarf with it trades instead."). The Build list's Runeforge "(no anvil)", and Add order's "○ Runed robe · no runeforge". The Thane's whim line with its progress ("10 of 10 deepfire in stock, met").
