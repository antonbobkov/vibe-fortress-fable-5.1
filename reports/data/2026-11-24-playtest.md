# Playtest 2026-11-24: a new player's first hour

**What:** the real game in a browser, played as a new player for about an hour of game screens, plus fast-forwarded days. **Commit:** `136f773d` (master, after T-696). **How:** `vite --mode test` on a local port, driven by Playwright through the keyboard and mouse at 1280 × 800 (the 36-column sidebar), state read through `window.__deepholm` and days skipped with its `step`. World seed `deep-522-1` (the default), region (42,30) "Ashford", a temperate forest with a brook and a lake, wealth 2, peril 1, temper 1. Screenshots are in [2026-11-24-playtest/](2026-11-24-playtest/).

**Played:** world generation and the site filter; the embark screen with the Farmstead preset; the first days (dig a hall into a hill with `d d`, a stockpile and its preset, chop, carpenter's workshop and still, a farm plot, a pasture, a hospital zone, door and table orders); clicking a dwarf, an item, the wagon, a stockpile tile and the crowded wagon tile, `Backspace`, `c`, `f`; Units' All tab; the log and its go-to and `[v]`; abandoning on day 21 and embarking again on the ruin (preset remembered, worn table, carpenter's wreck); fifty fast-forwarded days of the second fortress. No console errors or page errors in the whole session.

**Not reached:** an armed old trap shown red in a ruin (the presets carry one mechanism at most, a single trap is armed only one time in four, and a testing fortress leaves no ruin); a patient in the hospital (the supplies rows were checked empty).
