# Playtest 2026-11-25 (third): the mid game, Years 2 and 3

**What:** a Balanced fortress taken from 7 to 54 dwarves over three years, headless between the looks, then played in the browser in stretches. **Commit:** `24b4cf12` (master, after T-718). **How:** the three-year script's fortress (`threeYearsWorld` + `setupThreeYearsFortress`, the Balanced preset as that script buys it, a 48 × 48 temperate forest with a brook, wealth 2, **peril 2**) on the new seed `playtest-1125-p2`, driven headlessly to Day 1 of Spring, Year 4 with saves on days 60, 112, 150, 190, 224, 260, 300 and 336 ([drive.test.ts.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-3/drive.test.ts.txt), the event log [events.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-3/events.txt)). Each save was loaded into `vite --mode test` in headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 (the 36-column sidebar), driven by Playwright with the keyboard and mouse, and played on with `window.__deepholm.step` — without the script, so from the load on the player (me) made the decisions. The Summer 9, Year 3 save is [frostvault-y3-summer9.bin](2026-11-25-playtest-3/frostvault-y3-summer9.bin). Screenshots are in [2026-11-25-playtest-3/](2026-11-25-playtest-3/). No console errors or page errors.

**The three years in short ("Frostvault").** Population 7 → 15 (Year 1) → 22, 34, 41 (Year 2) → 52, 54 (Year 3): waves of 4, 4, 9, 12, 9, 12 and 6, the later ones at `WAVE_MAX`, as the wealth grew to 55,000 ☼ (at Summer 14, Year 2, 18,690 ☼ of the 32,353 were meals). Threats: five lone thieves (all withdrew), two bandit raids and two Gloam raids (11 and 16, all looted, no dwarf killed under the script's defence), four Gloam ambushes (all looted; **nine dwarves bled out from arrows**, the only dwarf deaths of the scripted run), no siege. Eight caravans, bought value 18 to 816 ☼. Steel: three steel greaves by Year 3 ("Make steel ×28 · waiting for bar"). No well was built (the script's cistern plan never reached it; every drink was alcohol). No masterwork, so no artifact moment. Two titles besides the Thane (Broker, Warden); Chief medic vacant throughout.
