# Playtest 2026-11-25 (sixth): the military and defence at peril 3

**What:** the military from the player's side: squads, their modes and gear, the alerts and shelter, doors and traps, a raid, an ambush, a siege, and the aftermath. **Commit:** `18997096` (master, after T-756/T-764). **How:** [drive.test.ts.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-6/drive.test.ts.txt) runs the three-year script's fortress (`threeYearsWorld` + `setupThreeYearsFortress`, Balanced, a 48 × 48 temperate forest with a brook, wealth 2) at **peril 3** on the new seed `playtest-1125-p3m` ("Stormvault") to day 260, saving on fixed days and at every warning. Saves were imported into `vite --mode test` in headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 (the 36-column sidebar), driven by Playwright with the keyboard and mouse, and stepped with `window.__deepholm.step`. From each load on, I made the decisions. Populations and events are in [events.txt](2026-11-25-playtest-6/events.txt). The save of the first raid's eve, Day 20 of Autumn, Year 1, is [stormvault-y1-raid-eve.bin](2026-11-25-playtest-6/stormvault-y1-raid-eve.bin). Screenshots are in [2026-11-25-playtest-6/](2026-11-25-playtest-6/). No console errors or page errors.

**In short.** The script's fortress grew from 7 to 47 by day 260: three raids (10, 13 and 15), four ambushes, a siege of 23 Gloam with trollkin (30 on the map), thieves and bandits. I played the first raid twice from its eve, the siege, an ambush and the last raid. Locking in under Hearth cost nobody. Each time I tried to use the squad, the gate and the alert keys worked against me. Lowering Hearth to Watch so the squad could reach its post opened the gate, and the raid walked in: all 13 dwarves were dead in two days. Twice a key meant for another panel lowered the alert during a fight: once to Calm under a siege, once to Watch at an ambush. A raid met on Calm killed eleven. The squad panel never told me where my soldiers were or what they were doing. Thirteen dead then lay unburied for up to twelve days, and nothing said why.
