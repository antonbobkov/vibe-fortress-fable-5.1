# Playtest 2026-11-26 (eighth): magic and the Deep

**What:** the deep end from the player's side: glowcap, the runeforge, a sanctum and runes; a stair to deepsilver beside the Sea of Fire; what the Deep sends up. **Commit:** `a21f95a2` (the T-791/T-792 merge, with T-791's lamp fix). **How:**
- **Choosing the map.** [seeds.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/seeds.test.ts.txt) generated the three-year region (48 × 48 temperate forest with a brook) at **wealth 3** for 30 seeds and listed every deepsilver tile. Deepsilver is always at z0–6 (`DEEPSILVER_Z_MAX` 6), so it is always 26 or more levels below a surface at z32. What a seed can change is where it lies. In playtest 7 it was in the Sea of Fire's rim at the map edge. On seed `pt8-9` it lies at z4–5, three tiles from the map centre, under the floor of cavern 3.
- **Getting there.** [drive.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/drive.test.ts.txt) ran the three-year script at peril 2 on that seed for 336 days. The fortress is "Frostwatch": 54 dwarves at Day 1 of Spring, Year 4 ([frostwatch-y4-spring1.bin](2026-11-26-playtest-8/frostwatch-y4-spring1.bin)). The script's event log is [events.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/events.txt).
- **Playing it.** I imported that save into `vite --mode test` in headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 and played it by keyboard and mouse through Playwright over CDP ([act.mjs.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/act.mjs.txt)). Days were skipped with the test hook's `step`. A stepping helper ([lib.js.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/lib.js.txt)) stopped at warnings, breaches and caravans. In the later stretches it raised the alert to Hearth at a warning and lowered it at the raid's end, as I had been doing by hand.
- **Fortress A, the descent:** Year 4 Spring to Year 5 Summer. I dug a stair from the cave-farm level (z23) to z4, broke into cavern 3, mined deepsilver and woke the Deep. Ending save: [frostwatch-y5-summer-deep-stalemate.bin](2026-11-26-playtest-8/frostwatch-y5-summer-deep-stalemate.bin).
- **Fortress B, runecraft:** the same Year 4 save played again. I traded with the spring caravan and built two glowstone lamps, a laboratory and a sanctum with a rune circle and two apprentices. Ending save: [frostwatch-b-y5-sanctum-no-glowcap.bin](2026-11-26-playtest-8/frostwatch-b-y5-sanctum-no-glowcap.bin).
- **Causes.** Two causes were traced headless from saves: [stair.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/stair.test.ts.txt) and [broker.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/broker.test.ts.txt). The cavern-3 water came from [water.test.ts.txt](2026-11-26-playtest-8/water.test.ts.txt).
- Screenshots are in [2026-11-26-playtest-8/](2026-11-26-playtest-8/). There were no page errors.

**My own mistakes, so they are not read as the game's:**
- In fortress A's first month I left the alert at Calm through a raid warning. Ten dwarves died.
- Twice my harness pressed Esc while checking whether the trade screen had opened. That closed the session. The second time, the Mountainhome caravan with 40 glowcap seeds left untraded.
- In fortress B my loop pressed [t] once, before the caravan had reached the depot, and never again. The Year 5 caravan left.

## In short

The deep end is reachable on a good seed, and its first steps read well:
- The stair tool says when a column crosses cavern air ("17 designated, 2 skipped (2 already open) · 13 out of reach").
- The deepsilver confirmation explains the price ([03-deepsilver-confirm.png](2026-11-26-playtest-8/03-deepsilver-confirm.png)).
- The Threats panel names where cavern 3 was opened ("3: open at (29,20,z7)") and shows the Deep's gauge.
- With T-791, the jeweler leaves the lamp's glowstone and the laboratory's. The Build row reads "Statue / lamp".

Two things then stop play cold, and neither says why:

1. **A cave crawler sealed in rock stalled a priority stair for 30 days.** It sat eight tiles away on the same level, cut off by rock. Every miner who stepped onto the stand tile "saw" it, dropped the job and fled. The next miner took the job up, and ten took turns. The Jobs panel showed "claimed", with no reason.
2. **The Deep's first answer stalled the Deep.** The emergence turns rock into air pockets, and the three hollow crawlers it sends up stand in them. They killed the miner who opened the vein. They then stood in their holes for a season, "Attacking". A squad under a kill order stayed "on the way". The same threat rule kept every miner away from the rest of the vein: "Nobody takes it yet … or a way clear of danger".

The Deep never went past level 1, and pressure stayed at 50 of 150. No wyrm or Maw came.

Runecraft stalled before its first session, for want of glowcap:
- One Mountainhome caravan brought 40 seeds. The other brought none, though glowcap seeds were requested at "high".
- The wild glowcap patches lay in a sealed pocket of cavern 1, and across cavern 3's lake: "745 gather jobs … no path".
- The sanctum read "Requirements met · Glowcap in stock: 0", and nothing said where glowcap comes from.

The spring caravan also met a raid's warning the next day in both years. One caravan, attacked, packed up two days after it arrived.
