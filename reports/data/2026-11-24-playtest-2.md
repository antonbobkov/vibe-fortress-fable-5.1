# Playtest 2026-11-24 (second): a Warband's first year on a wetland

**What:** the real game in a browser, played as a new player through a first year, with fast-forwarded days between the decisions. **Commit:** `d4b36941` (master, after T-705/T-708). **How:** `vite --mode test` on a local port, a headless Chromium driven by Playwright over CDP with the keyboard and mouse at 1280 × 800 (the 36-column sidebar), state read through `window.__deepholm` and days skipped with its `step`. World seed `deep-2346-1` (a new random seed), region (11,30) "Sedgefen": a wetland with a river, soil four levels deep and no surface stone, an `old_battlefield`, wealth 2, peril 3, temper 1. Preset **Warband**, plus a copper pick, 10 trout and a barrel of ale from the left-over budget. Screenshots are in [2026-11-24-playtest-2/](2026-11-24-playtest-2/).

**Played:** the filter and site panel; the embark (presets, supplies, animals); Spring 1 to Winter 15 (98 days): a hall dug into a silt hill, stockpiles, chopping, a carpenter's workshop, a still, a farm plot of turnips through three seasons, a squad on Train with a barracks, orders (barrels, bins, tables, chairs, door, chest, bucket, brewing), straw pallets from gathered reeds, a stair shaft that breached a cavern, a second shaft to rock, gathering, a fishing zone, a hospital zone, the Hillfolk caravan and the depot, two thieves, a bandit raid, an ambush, a Gloam raid of 11, the restless dead every autumn night, a soldier's death, mood breaks, starvation, two immigrant waves; then rename (Reedholm), Save, Load (world hash equal after the load), Settings, Abandon, the world map's ruin line and the Legends. No console errors or page errors in the whole session.

**The year in short:** the wagon's food (50 units) was gone by Spring 15; fishing and gathering carried summer; the caravan left without trading; from Autumn 13 to the end most dwarves were starving and one starved to death (Winter 7). Raids did no harm: the river between the south edge and the fortress turned back the Gloam raid, the bandits fled, the ambush withdrew. The restless dead killed Borin Wolfcloak and five dogs. Hall spirit was Unhappy from Spring 19 on.
