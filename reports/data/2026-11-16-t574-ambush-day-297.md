# T-574: the day-297 ambush on three-years-p4-1 (shift 0)

Replayed locally on T-555's code (`8baf7a1d`, the same as master `baf8da3d`) with the sweep's own loop (`test/perf/t574_raid_probe.test.ts`, `T574_PROBE=1`); the probe gives the sweep's 41 deaths and the same killer ids. A save at tick 355,200 (`PROBE_SAVE_AT`) replays the raid in 12 seconds (`test/perf/t574_replay.test.ts`) and gives the same 37 deaths.

## What came

- **Not a raid: an ambush.** No `ThreatWarning` and no `RaidArrived`: party 117442978, purpose `ambush`, arrived hidden on the north edge (x 2 to 9, y 0) at tick 355,200 (day 297, hour 0) and sprang at 355,255 (`AmbushSprung`).
- **Its size against renown.** Nine Gloam warriors and archers: `round(5 + 0.15 × 26.2) = 9` at renown 26.2 (52 dwarves), under the peril-4 cap of 10 (threat-schedules.md § Ambushes). As the table says.

## The fortress

- **The squad.** The script raised the hearth alert at tick 355,220 (it answers any party but a thief as a raid) and posted the squad on the gatehouse stair (28, 8, 32). Of five, one was in hospital and one asleep below; three went to the stair. Nothing to muster (the muster is for wild animals). When the gate locked, they stood between its two locked doors. They struck no blow: all nine Gloam lived through the raid, and the party's morale went from 75 to 100.
- **The gate and the second line held.** Both gate doors and the four doors of the second line locked by tick 355,700; the walls stood. No raider entered the gatehouse or the halls.
- **The civilians were caught outside.** 22 were on the surface when the alert went up, most on the row y = 6 between the gate and a workplace at (8, 5) by the map's north-west corner, where the ambush came. They turned for the gate; the ambush sprang among them. Their flee walks were served from 22's path cache, joined at the cached route's first tile, (18, 6): from (22, 6) a dwarf walked four tiles west towards the warriors at (16 to 18, 5) and then back east over the same tiles to the gate. Each threat scan stops the walk and asks again, and got the same route back, so for 400 ticks some twenty dwarves shuttled between x = 18 and x = 23 two tiles from the warriors. The gate locked on its grace (`ALERT_LOCK_GRACE_TICKS`, 400) with 25 still outside.
- **What killed them.** 37 between ticks 355,345 and 357,551 (days 297 and 298), 36 civilians and one soldier, 31 on the surface and 6 on the low ground at the map's north edge: struck down by warriors 20 and by an archer 1, bled out from arrows 14 and from warriors' blows 2.

## The fix and the replay

22 § Path cache now joins a cached route at its tile nearest the requester (within the route's leading run in the requester's chunk), and a requester on the route takes it from where it stands. From the same save, the ambush kills **3** dwarves in three days instead of 37; the civilians reach the gate before it locks.
