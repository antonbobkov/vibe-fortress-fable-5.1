# Playtest 2026-11-25 (fifth): a newcomer who has never read the docs

**What:** the game played as a complete newcomer, using only what it says on screen: the intro, the main menu, the tutorial, then New world with every default, about three weeks of a first fortress, each panel once, a small window, the mouse alone and the keyboard alone. **Commit:** `ceaa1543` (master, after T-749/T-750). **How:** `vite --mode test` with `?intro=on`, headless Chromium at 1280 × 800 (80 × 50 cells) driven by Playwright over CDP with the keyboard and mouse; state read through `window.__deepholm.text()`, and days skipped with its `step` once a decision was made. Tutorial world `tutorial-vale`; then the default seed `deep-5589-1`, the first land region east of the starting cursor, "Bitterbrae" (taiga, no surface water, peril 1), the Balanced preset unchanged, fortress "Stonewatch". Screenshots are in [2026-11-25-playtest-5/](2026-11-25-playtest-5/). No console errors or page errors.
