Over the 35 pairs of disjoint sets of four among the eight shifts, the two means differed by at most 75.25 living (24.85 on average) and 18 deaths (6.6 on average); the four-shift means ran 1089 to 1164.25 living and 93.5 to 111.5 deaths. On T-580's eight shifts the same pairs differed by up to 150.5 living and 25.25 deaths; on T-513's ten shifts of an older master, 96.25 and 26.25.

**The living narrowed by half; the deaths did not.** Peril 0 shows it most: its living moved 38 a sweep when each shift rolled its own waves and moves 7 to 9 now, since a peril-0 fortress rarely loses dwarves and its population is mostly the waves it rolled. The deaths are the timeline's raids and ambushes. The eight shifts here happened to be calm (sd 11.4), but the three other codes' sweeps ran 15 to 25 (T-681's shift 100 lost 157, `p4-4` 34 of them), and the pooled 18 is T-580's 16.8. A single ambush that finds the dwarves outside still moves a sweep by 20 to 40 deaths, and the shared rolls do nothing about that.

**The swing the orchestrator saw is noise.** Master's four sweeps of 2026-11-22 at shifts 0 to 150 pooled 112.5, 97.75, 98.25 and 120.25 deaths and 1116.25 to 1189 living. Two four-shift sweeps differ by 12.7 deaths (one standard deviation) by chance alone, so 22.5 is 1.8 of them; across four sweeps the largest gap is expected near 2 standard deviations. Nothing in those codes needs a cause found for its deaths.

## The tolerances and best figures

**Living: 100 to 80.** A four-shift mean of unchanged code stands 32 living from the eight-shift best (one standard deviation, pooled); 80 is 2.5 of them, about one false block in 160 sweeps. It is wider than the largest gap between two disjoint sets of four (75.25), the rule T-513 set.

**Deaths: kept at 30.** Its noise did not narrow (11 deaths, four against eight); 30 is 2.7 standard deviations. A tolerance of 25 would block unchanged code about once in 85 sweeps, and master's own sweeps of the day sit 5 under to 18 over the new best.

**The best figures and reference: reset to this master.** T-580's best figures (987.75 living, 131.88 deaths) were set when each shift drew its own rolls, and master had since moved 139 living and 29 deaths past them: its bounds (887.75, 161.88) let a loss of over 200 living or a rise of nearly 60 deaths through. The new reference is shifts 0 to 150 of `f83df33f`, 20 / 7 / 0 / 1151.75 / 98.25 with 0 extinct and 0 need deaths; the best figures are its eight-shift means, 1126.63 living and 102.5 deaths, as T-543 set them, better than T-580's on both; the bounds are 1046.63 living and 132.5 deaths. Both halves and the three neighbouring codes pass them (lowest living 1101.5, highest deaths 120.25).

**What that buys.** A rise of 30 deaths a sweep or a loss of 80 living is blocked half the time; 44 deaths or 121 living nine times in ten. Under the old bounds against today's level, a change had to cost about 59 deaths or 239 living before it was blocked at all.

## Worth tracing

Inside the bounds, a figure about twice the noise from the best figures is worth tracing by seed before it is accepted: **pooled deaths 22.5 or more over the best (125 now), or living 65 or more under it (1061.63 now)**. The verdict prints this as its own line (`SWEEP_TRACE`, `sweepTrace`); about one sweep in twenty-five of unchanged code will show it, and a look at the per-seed table usually finds one ambush in one timeline. Nearer than that is noise: two sweeps of nearly the same code at the same shifts differ by up to about 25 deaths and 75 living. By peril, a four-shift peril-0 living that falls 10 or more (twice 5.1) is real, since peril 0 is now a sharp gauge of the economy; peril-4 deaths moving under 21 and peril-2 deaths under 14 are noise.

Over all the seasons any of the twenty seeds could roll (the 200 that are not guaranteed, whether reached or not), 147 are under 700, against 140 expected (z +1.1).

So the seed set's fixed luck is mild overall. Three places stand out, each within the chance of 9 cells: every peril-2 fortress gets its first winter wave (7 of 7), peril 4 gets half of them (3 of 6), and peril-4's year 3 rolls a little high (19 of 24, the 78% T-652 saw). The peril-4 living is the figure this luck flatters most; a change is judged on the same rolls, so it does not bias a comparison, but the level of peril-4 living is these seeds' and not the game's average.
